7Yellow lycra, beginner course

Camila Rojas

Santiago

I stood up on the second morning and fell straight off. On the third I rode one all the way to the sand and screamed so loud Nico heard me from the car park.

Nico kept telling me to look at the beach, not at my feet. I forgot every single time until that third morning, and then it all happened at once.

My arms were useless for two days afterwards. I’d do it again tomorrow.

12Yellow lycra, beginner course

Tomás Fuenzalida

Rancagua

I’m 54 and was sure I’d hold everyone back. Nobody waited for me because nobody needed to. Same white water, same falls, same grin.

Javiera kept me on the inside with the 8'0" and let me take as many waves as my shoulders allowed. By the last morning I was the one telling the twenty-year-olds to paddle harder.

My wife has the video. I’ve watched it more than she has.

3Yellow lycra, beginner course

Hannah Weber

Leipzig

The water is 14 degrees and nobody pretended otherwise. The 4/3, boots and hood made it fine. I booked one lesson and stayed for five.

I had read about the Humboldt current and expected to suffer. Seba made me wear the boots even though I said I didn’t need them. I needed them.

Every morning the fog sat on the point until about ten, and then the whole beach turned blue.

21Pink lycra, family morning

The Muñoz family

Talca

Benja wouldn’t let go of his mum’s hand for the first half hour. By the end he was pushing his own board out and telling Seba which wave was his.

We came for the kids and spent the whole morning in the water with them. Seba put the four of us on two boards and swapped us every few waves, so nobody sat on the sand for long.

24Pink lycra, family morning

Gloria Sepúlveda with her granddaughters

Pichilemu

I grew up here and never once went in. I watched from the rocks with a thermos. This summer my granddaughters held the board for me.

Javiera didn’t make a fuss about my age. She showed me how to lie on the board and let the white water carry me in, and I did that eleven times.

The girls took a photo of me on the board. It’s on my fridge now.

31Blue lycra, back in the water

Viña del Mar

Eleven years off a board, two kids, one bad knee. I wanted someone to watch my pop-up and tell me the truth. Seba did. Second session: a green wave, a left, mine.

On the first session I was late on every pop-up and blamed the board. Seba filmed two waves on his phone, and I stopped blaming the board.

29Blue lycra, back in the water

James Carey

Cork

Last time I surfed was Lahinch in 2009. My paddling had gone, my timing hadn’t. They fixed my stance on the sand before we got wet.

Javiera saw that my back foot had crept forward over fifteen years and moved it back in two minutes on the sand. The water felt like home, which is not a compliment.
