    Things I use

    Six of the 22 things in the roll. If your machine needs something that is not here, ask me before you buy it.

    • Multimeter

      Autoranging, bought in 2007, with a continuity beeper loud enough for a bar.

      Nine faults in ten show up on the beeper before I open the manual. I replace the leads every year and nothing else.

    • Soldering iron

      60 watts, temperature controlled, with a chisel tip.

      Coil lugs are big and old joints are dirty. A small iron cooks the wire before the solder flows.

    • Contact file

      A flexible file for tungsten points and a burnisher for gold ones.

      Most dead features are one dirty switch. Sandpaper sheds grit and eats the plating; a file does neither.

    • Rubber ring kit

      310 rings in 14 sizes, sorted in a fishing-tackle box.

      New rubber changes how a machine plays more than any other repair, and it is the cheapest one I do.

    • Jumper leads

      Six alligator leads in six colours.

      To prove a coil or a lamp works before I blame the board. Six colours, so I can see which one I forgot to take off.

    • Notebook

      One page per machine, in pencil.

      What was wrong, what I changed, what I would check next. The owner gets a copy, because the next technician will want it.
