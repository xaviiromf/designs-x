Request a coaching set

A coaching set is one hour, online or in our room on Cerro Alegre. You play a first-to-five against one of us, then we go through the replays together and you leave with three things to practise.

It is free for players from the Valparaíso region. Everyone else is asked for a donation to the travel fund.
