Room for two kids and the weekly shop, on a bike that fits the lane

The Kade L2 is a front-loading cargo e-bike, built in Rotterdam. Here it is one system at a time: the box, the drive, the steering and brakes, and the size.

A box that carries 100 kg low and in sight

The box sits between your hands and the front wheel, 32 cm off the road, so the weight stays low and your kids stay where you can see them. The bench folds flat for crates, and both five-point belts clip into the frame, not the plywood.

Pedal like a normal bike, climb like a lighter one

An 85 Nm mid-drive motor and a stepless hub change gear for you as the lights change. A carbon belt replaces the chain: no oil on your trousers, and about 20,000 km before it needs a look.

Turns in a 4.6 m circle, stops in 5.5 m loaded

A steel rod under the box links the bars to the front wheel, so steering feels direct and there are no cables to stretch. Four-piston hydraulic brakes on 203 mm rotors stop a fully loaded bike from 25 km/h in 5.5 m.

2.21 m long, 64 cm wide, through a standard front door

That is shorter than most front-loaders and narrow enough for an 80 cm doorway. The centre stand lifts the front wheel and locks the steering, so the bike stays put while you lift a child out. The rear rack takes a second child seat.
