Tidewater Run, our newest 16-bit cartridge, ships on Friday

A tide-racing platformer by Clara Mendes and Rui Faria from Aveiro. You outrun the flood up the salt flats, and every level drains a little lower than the last. Battery save, 48 levels and a 32-page printed manual.

The first run is 1,200 cartridges at €44 each, in a smoked shell with a fold-out map of the flats. We post the first ones from Porto on Friday 13 November, and copies 1 to 200 come signed by both of them.

About us

Dust Jacket is four people in a workshop in Bonfim, Porto. Since 2019 we have put 31 games on cartridge, and every one is flashed, tested and boxed at our own bench.

The shop counter is open on Saturdays from 10:00 to 14:00, if you would rather collect than wait for the post.

Returns

Send back an unopened cartridge within 30 days and we refund the price. If a cartridge stops working in its first two years, we replace it and pay the postage both ways.

Email orders@dustjacket.example with your order number first, so we know it is coming.
