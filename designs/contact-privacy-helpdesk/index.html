Reach the desk without being overheard.

Kerckhoffs Desk is five volunteers in Lisbon who help journalists, organisers and small newsrooms lock down phones, laptops and accounts, for free. Signal is the safest way to reach us.

The rain behind the routes is the desk's traffic. Type a message and your words fall into the email lane as ciphertext.

  1. Signal

    The safest route for most people: encrypted end to end, nothing to set up, and messages can disappear after a week.

    How to check our key

    Anyone can publish a key with our address on it. The fingerprint is how you know the key is ours. It takes about five minutes.

    1. Get our public key from keys.openpgp.org (opens in a new tab) by searching for ola@kerckhoffs.example.
    2. Open the key's details in your mail app. In Thunderbird: Account Settings, End-to-End Encryption, then the key's properties.
    3. Compare all ten groups with the fingerprint on this page and on the poster at the open evening. Every character must match:
      • 7D3A
      • 91C4
      • E2B8
      • 0F56
      • A9D1
      • 4C7E
      • 38B2
      • F05A
      • 6E19
      • D4C3
    4. If one group differs, do not use that key. Tell us on Signal, and we will check it with you.
