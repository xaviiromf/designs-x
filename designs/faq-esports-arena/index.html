Tickets

Sorted before you travel.

Entry on all three days to any unreserved seat in blocks A to F, plus re-entry with your wristband. A weekend pass is £64.

Day tickets are £28 for Friday (quarter-finals), £34 for Saturday (semi-finals and the Iron Circuit top 8) and £34 for Sunday (both grand finals). Floor standing in front of the main stage is Sunday only and sold separately at £12.

Yes, once and free of charge, until Thursday 12 November at 23:59. Use the Change name link in your ticket email.

After that, the name must match your photo ID at the door, and the box office cannot change it on the day.

Rift Protocol is rated 16, and the broadcast shows realistic weapon violence. Under-16s need an adult (18 or over) in the same booking. Under-5s go free on a lap, with no seat of their own.

The Iron Circuit open bracket is for players aged 13 and over.

Doors

Friday from 16:00. Saturday and Sunday from 10:30.

Friday: doors at 16:00, and the lights go down for the first quarter-final at 17:00. Saturday and Sunday: doors at 10:30, first match at 11:30.

Gate 2 on Kiln Street is the quickest way in. Gate 4 is for wristband re-entry only. On Sunday, plan your way home around the late finish: see Will Sunday’s grand final run late?

One bag up to 40 x 30 x 15 cm, checked at the gate. Banners up to 1 x 2 m with no poles, power banks up to 20,000 mAh and sealed water up to 500 ml are fine.

Not allowed: air horns, laser pointers, outside food (except baby food and food you need for medical reasons) and cameras with detachable lenses. Your own controller for the open bracket is welcome: see Can I bring my own controller?

Yes, from Gate 2 to every block. Wheelchair spaces with a companion seat are in row 1 of blocks C and E; book them through the info desk.

Ear defenders are free at the info desk. The quiet room on concourse level 1 has a live feed and no crowd noise, and the main screen captions every caster segment.

Watching

Inside the bowl, blocks A to F.

Yes, until 19:00 each day. Keep your wristband on and scan out and back in at Gate 4.

A lost wristband is replaced once at the info desk, with photo ID.

Around 95 dB at the peaks, mostly match points and caster hype. Foam earplugs are free at every bar, and the quiet room shows the broadcast without the crowd.

Photos and short clips for your own socials are fine, with no flash toward the stages. Live-streaming the match feed is not allowed, because of broadcast rights.

The free Wi-Fi, KSA-Guest, is capped at 5 Mbps.

Saturday from 14:00 to 15:30 on concourse level 1. Queue tickets are handed out at the info desk from 12:00, one per wristband, 300 in total.

A best-of-five can run past 22:00. The arena has a hard curfew at 23:00: if the final reaches it, the deciding map is finished and the trophy ceremony moves online.

The last tram from the Kiln Street stop leaves at 23:40.

Playing

Iron Circuit open bracket, side stage.

Register online until Friday at 18:00, or at the player desk on concourse level 1 until Saturday at 09:30.

Entry is £10 on top of a Saturday or weekend ticket, capped at 256 players, double elimination. Pools start at 10:00 on Saturday on the side stage.

Yes. Wired pads and arcade sticks are checked at the player desk before your first match. Wireless pads must be paired by the tournament organiser, and turbo or macro buttons are not allowed.

Spare pads are kept at the desk in case yours fails.

You are called three times over ten minutes, on the side-stage screen and over the PA. After the third call the match is recorded as a loss and you drop to the losers’ side. Miss a second call and you are out.
