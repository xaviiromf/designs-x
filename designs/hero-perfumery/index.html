Forcing Shed smells of rhubarb pulled by candlelight.

Every February, Yorkshire growers pull forced rhubarb by candlelight in long, dark sheds. Our fourth scent, out 22 October, is that room: the sharp pink snap of a stalk, warm beeswax, cold earth underfoot. Eau de parfum, 50 ml, £118.

How it wears

  1. On spraying

    Rhubarb stalk, pink grapefruit and green leaf. Sharp and wet, almost sour.

  2. After 20 minutes

    The candle comes in: beeswax, a snuffed wick and rose.

  3. After 2 hours

    Orris and ambrette. Softer and a little powdery, like the inside of a wooden crate.

  4. After 8 hours

    Vetiver, cedar and musk, with a trace of cold soil. Close to the skin until the next morning.

Stalk heights over time are our perfumer’s estimate from blotter tests.

Seven declared allergens

These occur naturally in the bergamot, grapefruit and rose in Forcing Shed and are printed on the box: limonene, linalool, citral, citronellol, geraniol, eugenol, farnesol. If you react to any of them, try the sample on the inside of your wrist first and wait 48 hours.
