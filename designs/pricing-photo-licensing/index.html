Licence terms

  1. The licence. We give you permission to reproduce the archive’s scan of each plate on your invoice, for the use and licence named there. The glass and the scan stay with the archive.
  2. Credit. Print “Pyne & Daughters Plate Archive, Bristol” and the plate number next to the image or in the picture credits.
  3. Copies and editions. A print run counts every copy of one edition, reprints included. If a reprint takes an Inside page or Cover licence past its limit, we invoice the difference to the next licence up.
  4. Changes to the image. You may crop, resize and retouch dust, cracks and emulsion damage. Colourising, compositing, or removing people or objects needs our written consent.
  5. Uses not covered. No licence covers training or testing AI models, resale through stock libraries, or merchandise such as prints and calendars. Ask us about these separately.
  6. Plates still in copyright. Before you pay, we tell you if a plate is still in copyright. For those, we ask the photographer’s estate on your behalf and add their fee, if any, to the quote.
  7. Payment and cancellation. We invoice when we deliver the scan, and payment is due within 30 days. If your title is cancelled before it is printed, we refund the licence fee, less £25 for the scan.
