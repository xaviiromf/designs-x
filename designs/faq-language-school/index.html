Before you book

  • ¿Qué nivel necesito?

    None. Complete beginners start on the first Monday of each month, so the group starts from zero together.

    If you already speak some Spanish you can start any Monday; the placement test puts you in one of six groups, A1 to C1.

  • ¿Cómo funciona la prueba?

    A 25-minute online test of reading and listening, then a 10-minute video call with a teacher in the week before you arrive. Your group and your teacher’s name arrive by email the Friday before.

    If the level feels wrong after two days, tell your teacher. Moving groups on a Wednesday is normal.

  • ¿Necesito visa?

    Citizens of the EU, the UK, the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and most of Latin America enter Chile without a visa for up to 90 days, and a course of 12 weeks or less fits in that stay. Check the Chilean consulate’s list for your passport before you book.

    We email an enrolment letter with your dates and homestay address. Keep it with your passport in case border control asks.

  • ¿Cuánto sale?

    Intensive 20 group lessons a week
    USD 290 a week
    Intensive Plus 20 group and 5 one-to-one
    USD 410 a week
    Homestay breakfast and evening meal
    USD 210 a week
    Registration once, books and enrolment letter
    USD 60

    A USD 150 deposit books your place. The rest is due 14 days before you start, by card or bank transfer. What happens if you cancel or change dates.

    In Chile you will hear ¿cuánto sale? far more than ¿cuánto cuesta?

Arriving

  • ¿Cómo llego desde el aeropuerto?

    Our driver meets you at arrivals with your name on a sign: USD 95, about 1 h 45 min, at any hour.

    Or take the airport bus to Terminal Pajaritos and change to a Valparaíso bus, which leaves every 15 minutes. About 2 hours and USD 12 in all.

    Send us your flight number two weeks before, so your family knows when to expect you.

  • ¿Qué pasa el primer lunes?

    1. 8:30Breakfast at the school on Calle Templeman, with the other new students.
    2. 9:00Class until 13:15, with a 20-minute break.
    3. 15:00A teacher walks you round the cerros: the ascensor, a cash machine, the pharmacy, where to top up your phone, and which micro goes downtown.

    Tuesday is a normal school day, and by Friday most people order lunch without pointing.

  • ¿Qué me llevo?

    Houses here have no central heating, and from June to August evenings drop to about 5 °C: bring a warm jumper and slippers.

    Bring shoes with grip. The hills are cobbles and stairs, and your first afternoon is a walk up and down them.

    Sockets are 220 V, types C and L. A small gift from home for your family is a kind start, never expected.

Classes

  • ¿Cuántos somos por curso?

    Six at most, four on average. In January and February, our busiest months, we open extra groups rather than go past six.

  • ¿Voy a hablar chileno?

    You learn the grammar taught in Madrid or Mexico City, from Chilean teachers.

    From your second week, a Thursday class covers how Chileans really talk: cachai, al tiro, la once, the dropped s. You will understand the bus driver and still be understood anywhere else.

  • ¿Qué hacemos en la tarde?

    Free, apart from two included activities a week. On Tuesday a teacher leads a walk: the murals of Cerro Alegre, the Ascensor Artillería, or La Sebastiana, Pablo Neruda’s house. On Thursday there is a cooking class: pebre, sopaipillas, empanadas de pino.

    Saturday trips to Viña del Mar, Isla Negra or the Casablanca valley cost USD 25 to 60.

Living with a family

  • ¿Qué incluye la casa?

    A private room with a desk, your own key, Wi-Fi, breakfast, an evening meal and laundry once a week. The evening meal is often la once around 8 p.m.: bread, avocado, cheese and tea.

    Every home is 25 minutes or less on foot from school. We visit each family twice a year and ask every student about theirs on their last Friday. If it isn’t working, you can change families.

  • ¿Me puedo cambiar de familia?

    Yes. Tell Carolina or your teacher; no reason needed. We move you within two days at no cost. About one student in thirty asks.

After the course

  • ¿Me dan certificado?

    On your last Friday: a certificate with your hours and your CEFR level, signed by the director of studies.

    Intensive Plus includes DELE exam preparation, and we help you register for a DELE session in Santiago.

  • ¿Y si tengo que cancelar?

    Changing your start date is free up to 14 days before. If you cancel up to 14 days before, everything comes back except the USD 150 deposit.

    After that, or if you leave early, unused weeks become credit for any course in the next 12 months. If you’re ill and have a doctor’s note, unused weeks are refunded in full.
