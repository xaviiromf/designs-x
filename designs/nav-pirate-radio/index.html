87.9 MHz

On air

Static Harbour has broadcast on 87.9 since 1987, first from the wheelhouse of a trawler called the Marie-Jo, now from a shipping container on the Quai de la Joliette. Live programmes run from 07:00 to 02:00, and the archive fills the night and the afternoon.

Schedule

The same eight programmes every day of the week, so you always know where to find yours. Public holidays run the Sunday tapes instead.

    95.2 MHz

    Shows

    Three kinds of programme share the day. Every one of them is made by volunteers, most of them people who wrote in first as listeners.

    • Music

      Chiptune and city pop

      Container Sessions plays chiptune live from the studio; City Pop Tide and Chip Sea take the evening. Nine hours a day.

    • Talk

      Phone-ins and the harbour

      Fish Market Mornings has the auction prices and the weather for the boats; Night Ferry is the phone-in that began on the trawler. Six hours a day.

    • Archive

      Tapes from the shelves

      Old programmes played back by date, every afternoon and every night. Nine hours a day.

    98.6 MHz

    Archive

    The boat years are on cassette, labelled by date and shelved at the back of the container. Volunteers digitise 40 tapes every Tuesday. Everything since 2004 was recorded as a file.

    Cassettes
    4,180, from 1987 to 2004
    Digitised
    2,960 so far
    Played back
    14:00 to 18:00 and 02:00 to 07:00

    Ask for a programme by its date and we put it on Harbour Tapes within a month.
