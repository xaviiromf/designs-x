The story so far

Five chapters as a set list: sixteen steps, one lane for each chapter.

Scroll and the playhead walks the sixteen steps.

  1. Chapter 1 of 5

    Slides at the cousins' parties

    2009 to 2013, Lagos

    A borrowed slide projector and a shoebox of hand-painted slides at family parties in Yaba. I learned that a room dances harder when the light changes on the beat, not between beats.

  2. Chapter 2 of 5

    Shop windows and a ticket north

    2014 to 2016, Lagos to Leipzig

    I mapped shop windows for rent money, then moved to Leipzig for a media-art course. My first patch was a strobe that counted bars, so it never fired faster than the music.

  3. Chapter 3 of 5

    The basement residency

    2017 to 2019, Leipzig

    Every second Saturday in a basement under a print works. I added a second laptop and a mixer so the wall never went black between DJs, and started counting nights.

  4. Chapter 4 of 5

    Rooms without a crowd

    2020 to 2023, Leipzig and touring

    When the clubs shut, I built installations: a light corridor in a city library and a dawn piece on a riverbank. When the doors opened again, the festivals called.

  5. Chapter 5 of 5

    Sixty nights a year

    2024 to now, Leipzig

    Resident at Pegel, on festival stages every summer, with a pad bank I can play blind. Each winter I teach a two-day patching workshop for people who have never written code.

    Things I use

    Everything fits in one flight case and a backpack.

    • The video mixer

      Four channels, two buses. Channel 4 is always a black frame, so I can cut the room to dark on the drop.

      Read chapter 3, where it came in

    • The pad bank

      Sixteen pads in four rows, one row for each drum layer, mapped to patterns I can find without looking.

      Play the pads

    • Laptop one

      Runs the patch. It has no fan, so the booth microphone never picks it up.

      Read the patch source (opens in a new tab)

    • Laptop two

      Runs the same patch one bar behind. If the first one freezes, I fade across and nobody sees it.

      Ask about a two-laptop setup

    • The cable bag

      Two HDMI-to-SDI converters, a 30 m SDI run, a spare power strip and gaffer tape in three colours.

    • The house rule

      Never more than three flashes a second. The room should dance, not wince.

      1

      The rider

      What a room needs for one of my sets. If something here is hard, write anyway: most of it has a workaround.

      2

      Screen

      An LED wall or a projection surface at least 4 m wide. A plain white wall works. I bring a projector only if we agree it in advance.

      3

      Signal

      One SDI or HDMI run from the booth to the screen, under 30 m. I bring converters for either end.

      4

      Table

      120 by 60 cm beside the DJ, not behind them, with two power sockets and a clear view of the screen.

      5

      Light

      House lights off during the set. Haze is welcome. The light desk's strobes stay off unless we have agreed a cue.

      6

      Time

      Set-up 90 minutes before doors, an image check with the DJ, and 15 minutes to pack down after the last track.
