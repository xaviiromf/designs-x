Patch these four first

Crease is a 10 HP analogue wavefolding oscillator: a sine core, a six-stage folder and two CV inputs to move it.

Blue cables carry control voltage. Pink carries audio.

  • Six folds on one knob

    Blue cable from FOLD CV

    Turn FOLD and the sine picks up harmonics in six distinct stages, so the sweet spots are easy to find again. Patch an envelope into FOLD CV for plucks that open bright and close soft.

  • Fold it off-centre

    Blue cable from SYM CV

    SYMMETRY shifts the wave before it folds. Off-centre folds add even harmonics, the hollow, reedy tones a symmetrical folder never reaches. Send a slow LFO here and the tone breathes.

  • Holds pitch over five octaves

    Blue cable from 1V/OCT

    A thermally matched transistor pair tracks within 3 cents over five octaves. Every Crease is tuned by hand against a precision voltage source before it ships.

  • A clean sine beside the fold

    Pink cable from SINE OUT

    SINE OUT carries the untouched wave while FOLD OUT carries the folded one. Layer the sine underneath as a sub, or patch it into another oscillator’s FM input.
