236 neighbours paid for the panels

In autumn 2023 we offered shares at £50 each, from £250 to £20,000 a member. 236 of you put in £118,000, which paid for the panels, two inverters, the scaffolding and the grid connection. Every member has one vote, whether they hold £250 or £20,000.

288 panels went up in nine days

In March 2024 the baths closed the sports hall for nine days while 288 panels went onto the south-facing roof: 124 kilowatts at full sun. The pool trust leases us the roof for 25 years and pays only for the power it uses.

Last year the roof made 109,400 kWh

Each row on the drawing is a month, April at the top. June filled its row with 15,200 kWh; December lit four panels' worth with 2,300. Over the year that's what about 40 homes use.

The baths use two thirds of it, at 15p

Pumps, filters and the pool heat pump run all day, so the baths took 74,400 kWh straight off the roof at 15p a unit. That paid us £11,160 and cost them £8,928 less than the grid's 27p would have. The 35,000 kWh they couldn't use went to the grid at 5.2p. Together: £12,980.
