Prescriptions

How long does a repeat prescription take?

For: anyone on repeat medication

Order through the NHS App or your GP surgery a week before you run out. The surgery can take up to 2 working days to sign it. Once it reaches us electronically, we have it bagged by the next working day and text you when it is ready.

Takes
3 to 4 working days in total
Do I have to pay for my prescription?

For: adults paying in England

Most adults pay the NHS charge per item. You do not pay if you are 60 or over, under 16, or 16 to 18 in full-time education. You also do not pay if you hold a valid maternity or medical exemption certificate, or receive certain benefits.

A prescription prepayment certificate (3 or 12 months) saves money if you need more than 3 items in 3 months, or more than 11 in 12 months.

Check if you can get free prescriptions on the NHS website

Costs
NHS charge per item, or free if exempt
Bring
Your exemption certificate
Can someone else collect it for me?

For: anyone collecting for someone else

Yes. They give us your name and address. For controlled drugs, such as some strong painkillers, they show their own photo ID and sign for them.

Bring
Their photo ID, for controlled drugs
What happens if my medicine is out of stock?

For: anyone waiting on an item

We try our other wholesalers the same day. If there is a national shortage, the pharmacist agrees an alternative with your GP before anything changes, and we never switch strength or brand without telling you. You get a text when it is in.

Can I get treatment without seeing a GP?

For: anyone aged 1 or over with one of seven conditions

Under Pharmacy First, our pharmacists can assess you in the consultation room and, if you need it, supply prescription-only treatment for:

  • sinusitis, aged 12 and over
  • sore throat, aged 5 and over
  • earache, aged 1 to 17
  • infected insect bites, aged 1 and over
  • impetigo, aged 1 and over
  • shingles, aged 18 and over
  • uncomplicated urinary tract infections in women aged 16 to 64

You can walk in, or your GP or 111 can refer you.

Takes
About 15 minutes
Costs
Free consultation; the usual charge applies to any medicine supplied

Vaccines

Can I get my NHS flu jab here?

For: people the NHS offers it to

Yes, from early October while stock lasts. It is free if you are 65 or over, pregnant, have certain long-term conditions, are a carer, or live in a care home. Otherwise it is private. Book by phone, or walk in on Tuesday and Thursday mornings.

Takes
About 10 minutes
Costs
Free if eligible, £18 privately
Do you give COVID-19 vaccines?

For: people the NHS invites

During the NHS autumn and spring campaigns. Eligibility changes each campaign and the NHS contacts people who qualify. Book online or by calling 119, and choose Wharf Lane as your site.

Book a COVID-19 vaccination on the NHS website

Costs
Free
Can I have flu and COVID-19 jabs at the same visit?

For: anyone eligible for both

Yes. We give one in each arm. A sore arm for a day or two is normal. Paracetamol helps, and if you feel very unwell, call 111.

Do you do travel vaccinations?

For: travellers, by appointment

Our private travel clinic gives hepatitis A and B, typhoid, rabies and oral cholera, and can supply antimalarials after a consultation. Book at least 6 weeks before you go, because some courses need 2 or 3 doses.

Costs
£15 consultation, taken off your first vaccine
Bring
Your itinerary and any vaccination record

Delivery

Who can get medicines delivered?

For: patients within 3 miles of Wharf Lane

Anyone with a prescription with us. It is free within 3 miles; tell us once and we deliver every repeat. Further out, it is £3.50 per delivery.

Costs
Free within 3 miles, £3.50 beyond
When will my delivery arrive?

For: addresses within our weekday rounds

Our driver goes out Monday to Friday, 11:00 to 13:00 and 16:00 to 19:00. On the morning of your delivery you get a text with a 2-hour window.

What if I am not in?

For: anyone not home when the driver calls

We never leave medicines in a porch or with a neighbour unless you have named that person to us in writing. The driver puts a card through the door. We redeliver the next working day, or you can collect from the counter.

Can you deliver fridge items and controlled drugs?

For: patients on fridge lines or controlled drugs

Yes. Fridge items travel in a cool bag and are handed to you, not left. Controlled drugs need a signature at the door.

Bring
Someone to sign
