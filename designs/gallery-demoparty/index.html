The twelve winners of Rasterline 2026.

Rasterline is a demoparty held in Bergen every Easter. From 2 to 5 April, 97 entries ran on the big screen in six competitions and the hall voted. These twelve took the podium places. Pick a competition, or open an entry to see what it ran on and how small it is.

Entry rules for Rasterline 2027

Rasterline 2027 runs from Thursday 25 to Sunday 28 March in Bergen. You can enter from the hall or from home.

64k intro
One executable of 65,536 bytes or less. It must run offline and end by itself within 8 minutes.
4k intro
One executable of 4,096 bytes or less. No files other than the executable may be read.
Oldschool demo
Any home computer released before 1995, with no added memory or accelerator. One disk or tape image.
Pixel graphics
At most 320 x 256 pixels and 32 colours, placed by hand. Hand in four working stages with the picture.
Tracked music
One module of 4 to 8 channels, 3 minutes 30 seconds at most. Samples must be your own or free to use.
Wild
Anything that fits no other competition, shown as a video capture of 5 minutes at most.
Deadline
Upload by Friday 26 March 2027 at 22:00, Bergen time. Later entries are shown out of competition.
All competitions
One entry per group in each competition, never released or shown before the party.
