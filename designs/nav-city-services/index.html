Find a council service

Check, report, apply for and pay for Ollerby council services online. Most take under ten minutes, and you do not need an account.

Bins and recycling

Black and blue bins are collected on alternate weeks; food waste every week.

Check

Find your bin day

Your collection day is printed on the sticker inside your bin lid, and black and blue bins swap each week on that day. Bins must be at the kerb by 6am.

What goes in which bin

Blue takes paper, card, cans and glass or plastic bottles. Black takes everything else that is not food or garden waste.

Report

Report a missed collection

Call 0161 496 0300 by 6pm the day after your collection day and the crew comes back within two working days.

Report fly-tipping

Call 0161 496 0300 with the exact location and what has been dumped. Photos of addresses on letters or boxes help us trace who left it.

Apply

Order a replacement bin

Replacements are free if the crew damaged your bin. Otherwise a new bin costs £28, delivered within ten working days.

Book a bulky waste collection

We collect up to five large items, such as a sofa or a fridge, from outside your home for £35. Book on 0161 496 0300.

Pay

Pay for garden waste

A green bin emptied every two weeks from March to November costs £52 a year. Sign up by the end of February for the full season.

Council Tax

Bills go out in March. Pay in 10 or 12 monthly instalments.

Check

Find your Council Tax band

Your band depends on what your home was worth in 1991. This year band A pays £1,412 and band H pays £4,236.

Report

Tell us you are moving home

Call 0161 496 0300 at least two weeks before you move so we can close your account and send a final bill.

Apply

Single person discount

If you are the only adult living in your home, your bill is cut by 25%.

Council Tax Reduction

If you are on a low income or get benefits, you could pay less or nothing at all. Apply even if you are in arrears.

Pay

Pay your Council Tax

Pay by card on 0161 496 0300, by bank transfer to the account printed on your bill, or with the bill's barcode at any PayPoint shop.

Set up a Direct Debit

Choose the 1st, 8th, 15th or 22nd of the month. Sign up on 0161 496 0300; your first payment comes out at least ten days later.

Parking and roads

Resident zones R1 to R9 are enforced Monday to Saturday, 8am to 6.30pm.

Check

Roadworks this week

Mill Lane is closed between Canal Road and the bridge until Friday, signed via Station Road. Wharf Street is down to one lane.

Parking zones and times

Your zone number is on the signs at each end of your street. The six council car parks cost £1.40 an hour, free after 6.30pm.

Report

Report a pothole

Potholes deeper than 40mm are made safe within 24 hours. Smaller ones are added to the repair schedule for that street.

Report a broken street light

Every column has a number on a small plate at eye height. Quoting it lets us send the right crew first time.

Apply

Resident parking permit

A permit for your first car costs £64 a year and a second costs £96. You need your V5C and proof of address.

Blue Badge

A Blue Badge lets you park closer to where you need to go. We decide most applications within 12 weeks.

Pay

Pay or challenge a parking fine

A penalty charge notice is £35 if paid within 14 days and £70 after that. Challenging it within 14 days holds the lower price.

Planning

We decide most householder applications within 8 weeks.

Check

Search planning applications

Open applications and their drawings are on the board at the Civic Centre and in Central Library. You have 21 days from the site notice to comment in writing.

Do I need planning permission?

Many small extensions, loft conversions and fences under 2 metres do not need permission. Check before you build.

Report

Report building work without permission

Call 0161 496 0300 with the address and what is being built. We do not tell the owner who reported it.

Apply

Apply for planning permission

A householder application for an extension costs £258. Include a site plan and drawings of the existing and proposed building.

Apply for building regulations approval

Building regulations cover structure, fire safety, insulation and drainage. Our inspectors visit at each stage of the work.

Pay

Pay a planning fee

Use the reference on your letter. It starts with KP followed by the year and five digits.

Housing

Council tenants can report repairs at any time. Emergencies are made safe within 24 hours.

Check

Your place on the housing register

Your priority band, A to D, is on your acceptance letter. Band A households recently waited about nine months for a two-bedroom home.

Report

Report a repair in a council home

Call 0161 496 0300 about leaks, broken heating or faulty locks, or 0161 496 0471 out of hours. We make emergencies safe within 24 hours.

Report damp and mould

A surveyor inspects within 10 working days and tells you in writing what we will fix and when.

Apply

Join the housing register

You can join if you have lived in Ollerby for the last two years or work here. Ask for the form at the Civic Centre; it takes about 30 minutes to fill in.

Get help if you are homeless

If you have nowhere to stay tonight, call 0161 496 0471 now. If you are at risk of losing your home, contact us as early as you can.

Pay

Pay your rent

Rent is due every Monday, in advance. Pay by Direct Debit, card or at any post office with your rent card.

Leisure and libraries

All nine libraries are free to join. Central Library is open until 8pm on Thursdays.

Check

Library opening hours

Central Library opens 9am to 6pm, Monday to Saturday, and until 8pm on Thursdays. The eight branches open 10am to 5pm and close on Wednesdays.

Events in parks this month

Guided walks on Sundays at 10am, outdoor cinema on the last Friday and the Saturday market in Riverside Park. Most events are free.

Report

Report a problem in a park

Broken play equipment is checked the same day and taped off if it is unsafe. Call 0161 496 0300 and say which park and which item.

Apply

Join the library

Membership is free. Bring a letter or bill with your address to any library and leave with your card.

Pay

Book and pay for a sports pitch

Grass and all-weather pitches cost from £38 for 90 minutes. Book on 0161 496 0300, up to four weeks ahead.
