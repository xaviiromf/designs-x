I photograph the mammals of the Chilean Andes that come out after dark. Most of the work is waiting: camera traps left for weeks at 4,000 metres, and nights in a hide at −15 °C.

In June 2025 I left three camera traps by a latrine above Salar de Surire, where an Andean cat had been seen twice in ten years. Each small frame is one trigger, stacked by the night it happened.
