How to pack a handheld

Six steps, about ten minutes. Most damage we see in the post comes from a loose box, not a thin one.

  1. Take the batteries out. Tape the battery door shut. If a rechargeable pack is swollen, leave it out of the box altogether and read the swollen battery answer.
  2. Wrap it twice in bubble wrap, screen side in, so nothing presses on the glass.
  3. Leave cartridges and cases at home, unless a cartridge is part of the fault. Then bag it and write its name on the bag.
  4. Use a box 5 cm bigger on every side. Fill the gaps with scrunched paper until nothing moves when you shake it.
  5. Put a note on top: your name, your email and the fault in one sentence. Add your reply number if we gave you one.
  6. Post it tracked to Greyscale, Unit 7, Sheaf Yard, Leeds LS9 8QA, and keep the receipt until it's back with you.

We email you the day it arrives, and again with the quote.
