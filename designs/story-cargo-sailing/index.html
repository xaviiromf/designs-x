Le Havre to Santa Marta on the wind

Follow Voyage 14 of Alizé, our 72-metre sail cargo ship: 336 pallets and 14 crates loaded in Normandy, 5,040 nautical miles across the Atlantic, and 24 days at sea. The engine ran for nine hours, all of them in port.

Arrival in Santa Marta, 30 March

Customs boarded at 12:10. Unloading took two days. The cargo bikes came out first and were on the quay by lunchtime.

Going back full

On 3 April she sails for Le Havre with 4,140 sacks of green coffee from four cooperatives in the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta and 60 t of cacao from growers around Ciénaga. Every pallet position is taken.
