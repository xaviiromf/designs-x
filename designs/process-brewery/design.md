## Why it works

**The one bold idea: one sight glass, read the way a brewer reads it.** A single tall glass tube sits in a sticky stage beside the chapters, with a lit panel behind it and a litre scale plate (0 to 2,500 L) printed behind the liquid. As you scroll, strike water turns into opaque mash and the scale disappears. During the vorlauf the cloudy runnings clear to amber and the numbers behind the liquid come back into focus (a CSS blur on the copy of the scale seen through the liquid). The wort transfers to the kettle and boils with rising bubbles, runs through the chiller into fermenter 3 and goes hazy under a krausen head, then turns bright gold, moves to the bright tank and drains to empty as the cans fill. You judge clarity by looking through the glass, which is exactly how a brewer does it.

- **One state, one clock.** A paused GSAP timeline with six one-unit segments tweens a single state object (litres, temperature, gravity, EBC, clock, vessel, layer opacities, blur, krausen, bubbles). Its `onUpdate` writes CSS variables on the glass and writes readout text only when a snapped value changes. The glass, the readout, the stencil code and the torch reading can never disagree.
- **Contiguous chapter triggers.** Each chapter has its own ScrollTrigger (`top 60%` to `bottom 60%`). One driver trigger reads their measured start and end and sets `master.progress((i + local) / 6)`, so a fast jump up or down the page still lands on the right state. Chapter text always scrolls normally and stays readable; only the glass is scrubbed.
- **A brick wall and one lit vessel, both literal.** Kiln Row brews under a railway arch, so the ground is the arch lining: `--brick` (#3A3442), the purple-grey of Staffordshire blue engineering brick, with its courses drawn in CSS (40px courses, 120px bricks in stretcher bond, joints at 3% white, no images). Text is `--foam` (#F2ECDF, 10.2:1) and muted text `--mortar` (#B5ADBE, 5.5:1 on the brick, 4.9:1 on the form panel). The brushed-steel tones (`--plate` #A9B1AD with its brush grain, `--plate-lit`, `--backlight`, `--ink`, `--etch`, `--paint`, and a `--spill` of backlight round the tube falling to a `--vignette` at the panel's far edges) exist only on the sticky stage panel, so the vessel's steel skin and its backlit sight glass read as one lit object on a dark wall, and the glass is the brightest thing on the page. This is a chosen dark, not default 2: no near-black, and no UI accent at all.
- **Amber is content, not chrome.** `--wort` (#D88F2C) and its tints appear only inside the glass and in the can tops in the tray, because they are the beer. Every interface state is foam: primary buttons are a foam fill with brick text (10.2:1) and a brushed-steel sweep on hover (brick on steel 6.5:1), and the focus ring, the rail marker, the chapter tab, the reading ticks, the collection-day pill and the confirmation disc are all foam. That also keeps this section apart from process-smallsat, whose Kapton amber is its state colour.
- **Instrument typography without template chrome.** Gantari is the only text family: the headings at 800 with -0.015em tracking and 0.95 leading, chapter titles and panel headings at 650, body at 400 on 1.55, readout values at 500. Checked in Chromium on 2026-10-24: Gantari has no `tnum` feature ("1111" 62.56px against "0000" 102.73px at 40px, with or without `tabular-nums`). Reddit Sans passes the same check but is already used by hero-velodrome and testimonials-mountain-hotel, so Gantari stays and the live readout values sit right-aligned in a fixed slot (`--slot`, 16ch measured at the value size). Their right edges never move while you scrub; only the left edge of a changing number shifts. There is no monospace, no eyebrow, no middle dot and no arrow on links. The only uppercase is the stencilled vessel code (MT, KT, FV3, BT1), which is a real tank identifier and `aria-hidden`; the readable vessel name is in the readout. The code is capped at `clamp(3rem, 1.5rem + 4vw, 6rem)` in matt `--paint` (3.0:1 on the panel; decorative, large and `aria-hidden`), so it never competes with the glass.
- **Structure is information.** Chapter numbers exist because brewing is a real sequence. Hairlines exist only as real row boundaries (the batch summary, the brew-sheet tables, the scale ticks). There are four panels in all (the steel vessel panel, the reservation form, the dialog, the tray outline), and radius follows the object: the tube is fully rounded, panels are 6px (folded stainless), controls 4px, the can tray 3px.
- **Arithmetic that holds.** 1,080 L mash water + 1,600 L sparge - 400 L kept by 400 kg of grist - 30 L dead space = 2,250 L in the kettle at 1.043. A 150 L boil-off concentrates it to 2,250 x 43 / 2,100 = 46, so 1.046. 2,100 L - 120 L in the hop bed + 20 L yeast = 2,000 L in fermenter 3. 120 L stays in the cone, so 1,880 L reaches bright tank 1, and 178 trays x 24 = 4,272 cans x 440 ml = 1,880 L. (1.046 - 1.011) x 131.25 = 4.6%. These live in a `DATA` object at the top of the script, and `#selftest` asserts them, the £15-a-four-pack total, the figures printed in the summary, and that the glass readout at the end of every chapter matches that chapter's readings list.
- **Dates checked against the 2026 calendar:** Tuesday 6 October (brew day, Day 0), Day 7 is Tuesday 13 October, Day 16 is Thursday 22 October, and collection is Friday 23, Saturday 24 or Sunday 25 October.

## Motion

Tier: detailed. Easing family: `expo.out` (CSS `--ease-out: cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`) for anything that arrives, `power2.inOut` (`--ease-swap`) for state swaps, `power2.in` for exits, `none` for every scrub. The tube outline uses `power3.inOut` so the draw is visible rather than finished in the first frames. Everything is built inside one `gsap.matchMedia` with `motion`, `reduce`, `fine` and `wide (min-width: 992px)`. Lenis runs on `gsap.ticker` (`autoRaf: false`, `lagSmoothing(0)`), with `lenis.on('scroll', ScrollTrigger.update)`. Lenis `anchors` is off on purpose: one click handler eases every in-page link through `lenis.scrollTo` (duration `min(1.3, .65 + distance / 4000)`), offsets chapters by the sticky band on narrow screens, and moves focus to the target heading. Hidden or dimmed load states exist only under `html.js:not(.intro-done)` inside a `no-preference` media query; `html.js` is added only when all five libraries loaded. Cleanup removes the ticker, destroys Lenis and removes the pointer listeners. When a condition flips, the reader's scroll position is restored with a native `scrollTo` first and then `lenis.scrollTo(y, { immediate: true, force: true })`, so a new Lenis with a stale internal position cannot leave the page at the top.

## Do not touch

- **One state drives the glass.** Change the batch in `DATA` and in `buildMaster()`, never by animating the glass, readout or code separately. Keep every scrubbed tween at `ease: 'none'` and keep tweens on the same property from overlapping in time.
- **The litre scale is shared.** `DATA.scale` (2,500 L) maps to the range from the bottom of the tube to one tube-radius below its top. The ticks SVG, both copies of the scale numerals, the liquid clip (`clip-path: inset()` on `--lvl`), the surface and krausen wrapper and the torch all use that same range. The level is a clip, not a `scaleY`, so the numerals seen through the beer and the bubbles are not distorted.
- **Layer order follows the process.** Water, mash, cloud, wort, haze and bright are stacked in the order they first appear and only fade in on top of each other. Inserting a layer out of order breaks the reverse scroll.
- **Text stays readable.** Inactive chapters change colour to `--mortar` (5.5:1 on the brick), never opacity. Rail siblings do the same. Each chapter's readings list carries the state the glass reaches, and the readout carries the live values (with the Colour row, the clarity the torch also reports, at 992px and wider), so touch, keyboard and screen reader users lose nothing; the glass is `aria-hidden` and the torch line is `aria-hidden` too.
- **Steel only on the vessel.** The plate, grain, backlight, ink, etch and paint tokens belong to `.panel` and the glass. Putting steel on the form, the dialog or the ground turns the page back into a light steel page and the glass stops being the one lit object. Amber stays inside the glass and the can tops.
- **Narrow screens.** Below 992px the stage is a sticky band of 36svh (304px at 390x844) on the brick, with the steel panel holding a short glass and the readout, and the rail underneath, leaving 64svh for chapter text. The Colour and Clock rows are dropped from the band (they stay in every chapter's readings), the rail shows numbers only with the vessel and chapter name in each link's accessible name, and the current chapter name is printed under the rail. On landscape phones shorter than 520px the band stops being sticky.
- **Robustness.** If any library fails to load, `html.js` is never set: the glass shows bright beer at 1,880 L in bright tank 1 with a matching readout, every chapter is foam, the can tray stays hidden (nothing is canned yet in that state), and the anchors, dialog, stepper and form still work in plain JavaScript. Under reduced motion there is no Lenis, no SplitText and native scroll, and the glass jumps to each chapter's end state.
- **Headings start at h2.** The host page supplies the h1. The dialog title is an h2 in the top layer.

## Adapting

Map the `:root` tokens first, then `DATA` and `buildMaster()`, then the copy.

- **Another liquid or process.** Keep the glass and replace its layers (`.l-*` and their `--o-*` variables) with the stages of your material: a cider press, a kombucha ferment, a dye vat, a sourdough levain in a jar. Write each chapter's segment in `buildMaster()` as a few `to(at, duration, vars)` calls on the state, and keep each chapter's readings list identical to the state at the end of its segment (`#selftest` checks this).
- **Another brand.** Change `--brick`, `--brick-lit`, `--brick-deep`, `--brick-hi`, `--courses` (or set it to `none`), `--foam` and `--mortar` for the wall and the interface; keep a dark ground so the steel panel and the glass stay the one lit object. Change `--plate` and its companions only if the vessel is a different metal. Keep amber, or your product's own colour, inside the glass.
- **Retiming or removing motion.** Hover and press timings are the `--d-*` and `--ease-*` tokens. The load sequence lives in the `intro` timeline (positions 0, .15, .25, .3, .45, .6 and .8). Scrub positions within a chapter are the `at` values in `buildMaster()`. To drop the torch, delete the `if (fine && wide)` block, the `.torch` and `.torch-glint` elements and the `.torch-row` in the readout. To drop the slosh, delete `qSlosh` and `settle`. To drop the bubbles, delete `.bubbles`. To drop the can lids, remove the tray `ScrollTrigger` and the lids show seamed. Never remove the reduced-motion branch.
- **Copy length.** Chapter titles fit on one line at about 22 characters; chapter text works from 45 to 75 words. More than about 90 words makes a chapter taller than a screen on phones and the glass changes before the text is read.
- **Layout.** At 992px and wider the intro and chapters take columns 1 to 5 and the sticky stage columns 6 to 12: the steel panel fills the stage above the rail. From 992 to 1199px the tube and readout shrink; the torch still works there. The readout slot is 16ch; if your longest value is longer, widen `--slot` rather than letting values wrap. The outro is summary 1 to 4, tray 5 to 7, form 8 to 12, and stacks below 992px.
