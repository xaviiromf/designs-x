Booking and quotes 6 answers

  • We give you a fixed quote after a free survey, either at your home or on a 20-minute video call. The price comes from the volume of your things in cubic feet, the distance, the stairs at each end and how close the van can park, plus any packing you want us to do. It only changes if the inventory changes, so tell us if you add a loft or a garage before the day.

  • Yes, if you are moving out of a studio or a one-bedroom flat. Fill in the room-by-room inventory form we email you, add photos of anything large or awkward, and we quote from that within one working day. Anything bigger gets a survey, because a missed loft or a sofa that will not turn on the stairs is how moves overrun.

  • A deposit of 10% of the quote holds your date. The balance is due 3 working days before the move, by card or bank transfer, and we send a reminder a week ahead. We never take cash on the day, so the crew will not ask you for money and you have nothing to sort out while the van is loading.

  • Chains slip, and we plan for it. You can change your date once for free with 48 hours' notice. On the day, the first 2 hours of waiting for keys are free, then it is £65 an hour for the crew and van. If completion falls through overnight, we keep your things locked in the van for £95, or move them into our warehouse storage. A new date may also need a new parking suspension.

  • You can cancel at any time by phone or email. More than 7 days before the move, we refund your deposit minus a £40 admin fee. Between 2 and 7 days before, we keep the deposit. With less than 48 hours' notice, the charge is 50% of the quote, because by then we have turned other customers away for your date.

  • Every move includes goods-in-transit cover up to £50,000, and we carry £5 million public liability for your property and your neighbours'. Tell us at the survey about any single item worth more than £2,000, such as a painting, a watch or a bike, and list it on the inventory. Extended cover for declared items costs 1.5% of their declared value.

Packing 6 answers

  • Every booked move comes with 20 free double-wall boxes, tape and paper, delivered a week before. Extra boxes are £2.40 each, and wardrobe boxes, which let shirts and coats travel on their hangers, are £6 each to hire. When you have unpacked, we collect the empty boxes for free within 4 weeks.

  • Yes. A full pack happens the day before the move: a team of two wraps and boxes everything, and you keep out what you need overnight. If you would rather do most of it yourself, a fragile-only pack covers the kitchen, glass, china and framed pictures. Both are priced at the survey, from how many rooms you have and how much is breakable.

  • Write the room and the word “top” on two sides of every box, not on the lid, because lids disappear once boxes are stacked. Use the room names of the new house, not the old one. On the day we stick a matching room sign on each door at the new house, so the crew can put every box straight into the right room without asking you.

  • We cannot carry paint, gas bottles, petrol, lawnmower fuel or anything else flammable, and no fireworks, frozen food or pets. Use up or give away what you can before the day. Keep passports, house keys, cash, jewellery, medicines and important papers with you in the car, so they are never on the van and never in a box you cannot find.

  • Clothes can stay in solid wooden chests of drawers, which we wrap shut. Desks, flat-pack units and anything with drawers that slide out must be empty, because they twist when carried. Switch off and defrost the freezer 24 hours before the move, mop out the water and leave the door ajar to dry, so nothing leaks onto your other furniture in the van.

  • Yes. The crew takes apart beds, wardrobes and dining tables and rebuilds them at the new house, keeping the fixings in labelled bags. Flat-pack that has already been built twice may not survive a third time, and we will tell you so at the survey. We do not take down wall-mounted TVs, so ask whoever fitted yours to unmount it first.

Moving day 6 answers

  • Between 8 and 8:30am. The crew lead phones you 30 minutes before they reach the door, so you know who is coming. A two- or three-bedroom move gets a crew of three in a 3.5-tonne Luton van with a tail lift. Keep one clear path to the front door, and they will walk the rooms with you before lifting anything.

  • You need one if the van cannot park within 15 metres of your door. The Luton needs 12 metres of kerb, plus room to lower the tail lift. Bristol City Council asks for about 10 working days' notice, so tell us as soon as you have a date. We apply for you for £35 plus the council's fee, and put out the signs.

  • We move upright pianos, with a fourth person and a piano skid. On the ground floor with no steps it costs £120 on top of your quote; stairs are priced at the survey once we have seen the turns. Grand pianos need a specialist team, which we book for you and fit around your move. Have it tuned a few weeks later, once it has settled.

  • No. Our insurance covers the crew only, and the cab seats three. Follow us in your car or by taxi, and the crew lead will give you their mobile number so you can meet at the new house. If you do not drive, tell us at the survey and we will plan the unloading around when you can get there.

  • Tell the crew lead before they leave, and they will write it on the delivery sheet that you both sign. Then send us photos within 7 days, as set out in how to make a damage claim. Things you packed yourself are covered only if the box is visibly crushed or torn, so stand plates on their edges and fill every gap.

  • Neither is expected. Tea, coffee and water are always welcome, especially on hot days or long carries. The crew takes a 30-minute lunch break and brings their own food, usually once the van is loaded. If you want to thank them, a review that names the crew lead means a lot, and we pass every one on.

After the move 6 answers

  • Yes. We load your things into sealed wooden containers in our St Philips warehouse, which is alarmed and monitored. Each container holds 250 cubic feet, roughly a one-bedroom flat, and costs £24 a week, with a 2-week minimum. You can visit or take things out with 48 hours' notice, and we deliver from storage when your new home is ready.

  • Yes, free of charge within 4 weeks of your move. Flatten the boxes, keep the paper in one bag, and ring the office to book a collection slot. We reuse the boxes that are still sound and recycle the rest. After 4 weeks you can still drop them at our St Philips warehouse during office hours, Monday to Saturday.

  • We are a licensed waste carrier, so we can take things you are not keeping on the day of your move. It costs £45 per item of furniture and £30 per mattress, and we send working items to a reuse charity where we can. We cannot take paint, chemicals, gas bottles or garden waste; the council recycling centre takes those.

  • Email photos of the damage and the number on your delivery sheet to the office within 7 days of the move. We reply within 5 working days. Where something can be repaired, we send a repairer at our cost; where it cannot, we replace it or pay its current value, which is less than the price you paid when it was new.

  • We move it, and fit your transit bolts first if you still have them, so the drum cannot swing. Plumbing it in at the new house needs a plumber, so we leave the machine in place with the hoses taped to the back. If you have lost the bolts, buy a set for your model before the day; they cost a few pounds.

  • No. Drilling into walls is outside our insurance, because of hidden pipes and cables. We rebuild the furniture we took apart, and we lean pictures and mirrors against the wall in the right rooms. Ask the crew lead for the name of a local handyperson; many of our customers in Bristol use the same two.
