The story so far

Five rooms, from 2013 to now

  1. Room 1 2013 to 2015

    I walked into walls for a living

    My first job was testing at Cardumen, a studio of twelve in Montevideo. I checked Marea Baja by running at every wall in it and filed 1,140 collision bugs. That is where I learned what a level is under the art: boxes.

  2. Room 2 2015 to 2017

    A ladder is a promise

    Cardumen let me build nine screens of Faro Arriba, a vertical platformer about a lighthouse keeper. My first ladders led nowhere worth going. Now I show the top of every climb before the player starts it.

  3. Room 3 2017 to 2019

    Fourteen floods

    I moved to top-down adventures at Dos Orillas. The flooded archive in Agua Quieta took fourteen layouts before players could read the water level without a line of text. The fifteenth shipped.

  4. Room 4 2019 to 2022

    On my own

    I went freelance with one client and four months of rent. Small studios kept hiring me for the same thing: the first hour, where a game has to teach itself. Eleven games so far.

  5. Room 5 2022 to now

    The room nobody needs

    These days I am asked for openings and for secrets. Kiln Road hides 31 rooms, and players took 16 days to find the last one. Twice a year I teach a short grey-box course in Montevideo.

Six rules I work by

  1. 1The first room teaches the whole game.

    Everything the player will need in hour ten should be visible, in small, in the first thirty seconds: a jump, a ladder, something out of reach. Paint a first room yourself

  2. 2Show the key before the lock.

    A lock met first is an obstacle. A lock met after you have walked past its key is a plan. Walk the five rooms again

  3. 3Difficulty is a staircase with landings.

    A curve that only climbs wears people out. Every third or fourth level should be easier than the one before it. Find the landings in the curve

  4. 4Grey box until it is fun.

    If a room is dull as plain boxes, lighting will not save it. I ask for art only after someone who is not me has played the grey box. Strip a room to its boxes

  5. 5Secrets pay curiosity, not patience.

    A secret behind a wall you must hit forty times rewards stubbornness. One behind a wall that looks slightly wrong rewards looking.

  6. 6Leave one room nobody needs.

    A dead end with a view, a bench, a strange statue. Rooms without a job are where a level starts to feel like a place. Ask me for one
