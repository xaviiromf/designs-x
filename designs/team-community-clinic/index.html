Who you’ll see at Saltmarsh Road

Fourteen of us run this clinic, most of us part-time. Choose a name to see what that person can help with, then ask for them by name when you book.

Book or change an appointment: 01632 960 412 (lines open at 8 am)

Hours are in 12-hour time, so 1–8 means 1 pm to 8 pm. A dot means not in that day.

Need an interpreter or a longer appointment? Say so when you call. Interpreters are free.

Pick a day to see who is in (changes the board below)

Open 8 am to 6 pm on weekdays, until 8 pm on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and 9 to 12 on Saturdays for walk-ins. Call at 8 for same-day appointments with Bernadette or Dr Okafor.
