The programme

91 films, 23 of them world premieres. Here are six to start with; the full list is in the printed guide, free at every venue from 1 February.

  • Sat 6 Mar 19:30 Opening night

    Keeper

    Ines Halvorsen, director. Norway, 84 min

    Harbour Hall

    Arne Solli has kept the light on Sørholmen for 31 winters. In April the coastal authority switches it to automatic. Halvorsen spent his last winter with him, with one 16mm camera and 400 feet of film a week.

  • Sun 7 Mar 14:00

    Ward 9, Nights

    Femi Adeyemi, director. United Kingdom, 97 min

    Leaf Street Cinema

    Three nurses on a stroke ward in Leeds through one winter of night shifts. Filmed with the consent of every patient who appears, and shown to them first.

  • Sun 7 Mar 21:00

    Salt

    Camila Rojas Vergara, director. Chile, 72 min

    The Ropewalk

    The Pavez family harvest salt by hand from the Cáhuil lagoon, as four generations did before them. This year the sea comes in a month early.

  • Tue 9 Mar 18:15

    Four Hundred Letters

    Tomasz Wierzbicki, director. Poland, 88 min

    Corn Exchange

    In December 1981 a young postman in Białystok kept a sack of letters he was told to burn. Forty-five years later he goes looking for the people they were written to.

  • Wed 10 Mar 20:45

    Hold Music

    Dana Whitlock, director. United States, 61 min

    Leaf Street Cinema

    One eleven-hour shift in a debt-collection call centre in Tucson, told only through the calls. No faces until the last minute.

  • Sun 14 Mar 19:00 Closing night

    Home Movies of the Alder

    Aldermouth Film Archive and 214 local families. United Kingdom, 79 min

    Harbour Hall

    Sixty years of the town’s home movies, donated this summer and cut into one evening.
