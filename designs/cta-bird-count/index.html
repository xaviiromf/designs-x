Take a square for the winter bird count

On Saturday 9 and Sunday 10 January, the Wenning Vale Ornithological Society counts every bird in the 25 tetrads (2 km squares) of the vale. Seventeen already have a counter. Pick one of the eight yellow tetrads, walk it for two hours between 8am and 3pm, and send us your tally.
