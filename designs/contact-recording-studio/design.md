## Why it works

**The bold idea: send the enquiry by recording it.** The send key is a tape transport. It holds a tape reel with a three-window aluminium flange, and a tape path runs from the reel across the key to a guide roller. Press and hold, and tape winds on: an oxide-brown pack (a thick ring, `stroke-dasharray` equal to its circumference) fills around the hub at constant speed. The spokes come up to speed like a capstan, and the VU meter beside the studio name rises to 0 VU and stays there, so the meter reads as the recording level. The label swaps to "Keep holding". When the reel is full, the Rec LED lights amber and the label reads "Recorded". The fields fade, and a cream tape-box label is revealed in their place. It is printed like a real one, with the studio's header, "Reel 1 of 1", a ruled grid of Artist, Project, Session, Room, First day and Days, tape-format boxes ticked in pen, and a boxed Notes area holding the estimate and when the studio will reply. Release early and the tape rewinds: the pack drains, the spokes run back, the needle drops, and the status line says "Released early. Hold until the reel is full."

- **It follows the brief's transport.** The pack's `stroke-dashoffset` goes from C to 0 linearly with the fill (ease none, 1.0s). The hub angle is `540° × p²` (power1.in), so it starts slow and speeds up. The needle angle goes linearly from rest (−20 VU) to 0 VU with the fill. On early release, the dash drains in 0.25s (power2.out), and the spokes and needle run back on their own 0.4s power2.out tweens from wherever they are. An earlier build replaced this with take-up reel physics (a pack whose area grows linearly, with the hub slowing as it fills). Review sent it back to the brief: a capstan coming up to speed reads as "recording starts", and a reel that slows reads as running down.
- **The meter is a real VU scale.** Ticks at −20, −10, −7, −5, −3, −2, −1, 0, +1, +2 and +3 are spaced by voltage (`10^(dB/20)`) across ±48°, so 0 VU sits at about 18° right of centre, as on a real meter, and the red zone runs from 0 to +3. It moves only to report something. On load it kicks once to −3 VU and falls back to its peg, like a calibration check. It twitches to −18 VU when the send key is hovered or focused (the input is armed). It reads the level during a hold and stays at 0 VU while the enquiry is sent. It has no idle motion, no pointer ballistics and no audio.
- **Nothing records an invalid take.** On press, the form is validated first. If anything is wrong, the reel does not move, each bad field opens its message, and the status line names them all ("3 fields need fixing: Email, First day, About the songs."). The messages push the key down under a finger that is still pressing, so the tap's click is swallowed wherever it lands, and only then does focus go to the first bad field.
- **The result takes the form's place.** The form and the label share one grid cell. Each is hidden with `inert`, `aria-hidden` and `visibility`, so the well keeps the form's height during the swap. The label is revealed at the bottom of the well, where the send key was. Then the well's height follows it (0.6s, power3.inOut) while the page scrolls by the same curve, so the label glides up into view instead of the page jumping. "Edit enquiry" grows the well first under the label, then closes the label and fades the form back in.
- **The result is useful, not a thank-you.** Estimate = (room day rate + £60 if recording to tape) × days, so live room, 3 days, tape is £1,440 before VAT. The reply time follows the phone hours: before 17:00 Monday to Saturday it says "by 18:00 today", otherwise "by 12:00 on Monday" (or the next working day). A visually hidden live region announces "Enquiry sent. Estimate … Reply by …" once the label is open.
- **Colour means something.** A dark ground is a choice here, not default 2 (near-black with one bright accent). The ground is the olive hessian on the studio walls, the well is a raised baffle, and there are two warm accents with separate jobs. Warm white is the working light: text, focus rings, the armed field rule and the lit LEDs on the push-button caps. Amber (`--lamp`) is the Rec lamp alone, lit only when a take is recorded. Red (`--rec`) means "in the red": field errors and the meter's 0 to +3 zone. A successful recording therefore never lights in the error colour. Cream is used only for the tape-box label, so the payoff is the one warm paper surface on the page.
- **One family, three widths.** Zalando Sans at width 125 gives the wide, flat grotesque of 1970s hi-fi and tape-machine panels for the studio name, the heading and the label title. The same family at 100 is for reading and typing, and at 85 and 600 for console-style legends and the label's printed captions. No monospace anywhere: its tabular figures turn into a monospace-style set, so prices and the estimate use the proportional figures.
- **Structure is information.** Field rules mark real field boundaries and draw in from the left after the well opens. The rate rules draw before their rows, and the label's ruled grid draws before its values are written in. Room caps carry the fact a band chooses by (the room's size and what it fits) as a caption, not a meta string. The channels are a plain definition list on the ground, not cards.
- **The reel moves only when someone holds it.** There is no idle spin, scroll spin or magnetic pull. When the key scrolls into view, its flange draws round once and the Rec LED blinks at a third: record ready. Hover answers through the object: the flange brightens, the Rec LED arms and the meter twitches. A valid press sinks the key to 0.985.
- **Every rate row does something.** Pointing at a row steps the others back to the secondary colour (a colour switch, not opacity, so they stay at 6.2:1) and swaps its price for "Choose". On touch, where there is no hover, each row's detail ends "Tap to choose." Clicking a row sets the room on the form (the control-room row also picks "Mixing your files"), scrolls to "Which room?" and puts focus on the checked cap, whose LED pops.
- **Channels first on phones.** At 390px the email, phone and address come before the ten fields, so a call is one tap away. From 992px wide and 740px tall, the heading and channels column is sticky (plain CSS) while the form scrolls past.

## Do not touch

- **Validate before recording.** `pointerdown` validates first and returns before the reel moves if anything is wrong. The capture-phase click swallow after an invalid press stops the tap from landing on a field or radio that the opening messages moved under the finger. Keep the 1.0s hold under reduced motion (it is feedback); only the spin goes.
- **The keyboard path.** The send key is `type="submit"`. A real pointer's click (`detail > 0` with a `pointerType`) is cancelled, because the hold handles it. Enter, Space, Enter in a field, a programmatic `.click()` (`detail 0`) and a synthetic click from assistive tech or voice control (no `pointerType`) all go through `submit`, which validates, then fills the reel in 0.35s and sends. check-flows depends on this.
- **Touch guards on the send key only.** `touch-action: manipulation`, `user-select: none`, `-webkit-touch-callout: none` and a cancelled `contextmenu`. Pointer capture keeps the hold if the finger drifts; `pointercancel` rewinds.
- **The transport functions** `dashAt`, `spinAt`, `needleAt` and `vuDeg`, and the two `.wind` groups (flange and hub), which only the hold writes. Do not add idle, scroll or pointer spin to them. The pack's dasharray (109.96) is its circumference at r = 17.5; change both together.
- **Three label spans, each in its own mask.** "Hold to send enquiry" wraps to two lines on phones. A shared mask let pieces of the other labels show during the swap. `.is-full` is added before `.is-holding` is removed, so the label goes from "Keep holding" straight to "Recorded".
- **One cell for form and label.** Do not go back to `hidden` on the label: the well would collapse before the reveal and the page would jump. `[hidden]` is used only on the form after the label is fully open, and that is what lets the well's height tween to the label.
- **Static labels above every field.** Floating labels were removed on purpose: they collide with the date input's format text and hide the hint while typing.
- **Errors never shake.** Messages open with `grid-template-rows: 0fr → 1fr` and `visibility`, carry text, and are linked with `aria-describedby`; `aria-invalid` is set per input, and on every radio of a group.
- **Hidden starting states only under `html.js`** (added only when GSAP, ScrollTrigger, SplitText and Lenis all loaded), and only with motion allowed. Each group (`in-load`, `in-well`, `in-rules`, `in-rates`, `in-send`) is released by its own class. A 3s failsafe releases the heading and the well even if SplitText fails. Without the CDN, the whole form, hold, meter, label and rate rows work, with CSS transitions only.
- **Contrast.** HV7 dims by switching to `--grille`, never by opacity (chalk at .45 fell to 3.6:1). Chalk is 11.9:1 on hessian and 9.8:1 on baffle; grille 6.2:1 and 5.1:1; rec 5.9:1 and 4.8:1; lamp 8.3:1 on hessian (graphics). Ink is 11.6:1 and ink-soft 5.5:1 on the label. Tape (3.6:1 on hessian) is graphics only.

## Adapting

1. **Data.** Change `RATES`, `TAPE_DAY`, `ROOMS` and `SESSIONS` at the top of the script, and the matching radios and rate rows in the HTML (each row's `data-room` must match a room radio's `value`). `replyText()` holds the opening-hours rule (Monday to Saturday, cut-off 17:00); change it with the phone hours in the channels. The label's tape-format boxes are `li[data-tape]` in the HTML.
2. **Another object that fills.** Keep the pattern: a physical thing fills while you hold, and the result is the record you would write when it is full. A kiln coming up to temperature, a cask filling, a spool of thread. Swap the reel SVG and the meter for that object's gauge. Keep the validate-first rule, the rewind on early release and the keyboard path.
3. **Copy.** The H2 names what is being booked in the subject's own words ("Book days on the 24-track"). The intro says what happens next and what is not yet promised. Button text is the action ("Hold to send enquiry"), and errors say how to fix the field. The result title keeps the same verb as the button ("Enquiry sent").
4. **Tokens.** Map the brand by role: a ground, a raised surface, one working light that also serves as the focus ring, one lamp colour kept for the success state, a secondary text colour at 4.5:1 or better on both darks, an error colour at 4.5:1 or better on both darks, and one paper colour kept for the result only.
5. **Layout.** One column below 992px, with channels before the form. From 992px, the aside takes columns 1 to 5 and the form well columns 7 to 12. The aside is sticky when the viewport is at least 740px tall (raise that query if the aside grows). The name and meter share a row capped at the intro's 46ch. Rate rows stack name and price over the detail below 720px.
6. **Retiming or removing motion.** CSS timing tokens: `--dur-*`, `--t-*` and the `--ease-*` curves. The script reads `--t-hold` (pointer fill), `--t-key` (keyboard fill), `--t-rewind`, `--t-spinback`, `--dur-fade`, `--dur-open`, `--dur-close` and `--dur-fit`, so changing those tokens retimes the hold and the label. To remove an effect, delete its block in the motion layer: the load (head rise, well clip, the line reveals, the channels' masked rise, the meter kick), `ScrollTrigger.batch('.ctl')`, the `.send-row` trigger, the rates trigger, or the send key's hover and focus twitch. Hover, focus and press responses are CSS transitions on the `--dur-*` tokens. Removing the four CDN tags gives the reduced-motion page with CSS-only feedback.
7. **Stack translation.** Keep the form a real form with `novalidate` and a submit handler, the result in the same grid cell as the form, the radios real inputs inside the caps, and the rate rows real buttons.

## Motion

House easing: expo.out for entrances and reveals, power3 for follows, the label closing and the well's height, a back-out curve only for LED pops, and elastic only for the needle settling on its peg. Everything is built inside `gsap.matchMedia` with `motion`, `reduce`, `fine` and `wide` conditions. Lenis (lerp .1, `autoRaf: false`, `anchors: false`) runs on `gsap.ticker` with `lagSmoothing(0)`. The hold's fill, spin and needle are short `requestAnimationFrame` loops in the plain layer, so they work without the CDN, and nothing runs between holds. GSAP takes the needle only for the load kick and the hover twitch, and a hold kills those tweens. The section has no menu or dialog, so Lenis never stops; the hook is commented where an adapter would add `lenis.stop()` and `lenis.start()`. The load score plays once. The cleanup removes listeners and the ticker callback, destroys Lenis, kills the tweens, clears inline styles and releases every hidden state.
