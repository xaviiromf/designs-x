    How we test

    A recipe is listed only after it passes four steps.

    1. Mix a 1,000 g test batch of the base and weigh each colourant addition.
    2. Dip tiles on three clay bodies, B-Mix 5 among them.
    3. Fire to cone 6 in oxidation, in the same kiln load.
    4. Check all three tiles against the recipe. If one is off, the recipe is not listed.
