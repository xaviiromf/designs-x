Hintline is a volunteer phone line and text chat in Utrecht for spoiler-free hints in point-and-click and text adventures from 1985 to 1999. We answer every evening from 18:00 to 23:00 CET, and we only go as far as you ask: a nudge, a hint, or the answer.

1Calling the line

  • Nothing on top of your normal rate. The number is an ordinary Utrecht landline, so most Dutch phone plans include it; from abroad, your provider's international rate applies. The text chat is free.

  • Every evening from 18:00 to 23:00 CET, weekends included. We are closed on 24, 25 and 31 December. The lamp on the telephone tells you, from your own clock, whether the line is open right now.

  • No. We do not record calls, and the chat keeps no history. The volunteer notes only the game and the puzzle, never your name or number, so we can see which puzzles people get stuck on most.

  • Four volunteers answer at once. If they are all on a call, you hear a short tune and stay in the queue; most waits are under five minutes. If you would rather not wait, ask for a call back and we ring you in the half hour you choose.

2How hints work

  • A nudge tells you where to look: "Have you looked closely at the fireplace?" A hint tells you what to do, but not how: "Something in the fireplace can be moved." The answer is the full solution, step by step.

3Games we know

  • Point-and-click and text adventures released between 1985 and 1999, on home computers and early consoles. Our shared notes cover 312 games, and each of them has been finished by at least two volunteers.

  • Call anyway. Puzzles from those years often share their logic, and a volunteer can usually reason it out with you. If nobody knows the game, we write it down, play it, and can ring you back with notes within two weeks.
