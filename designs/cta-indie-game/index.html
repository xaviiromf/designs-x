Wishlist Noctiluca, then play the first bay tonight.

Noctiluca is a puzzle game about a harbour at night. The water only lights up where something moves, so every push of your boat shows you a little more of the currents. The free demo is Bay One: 24 puzzles, about an hour.

Wishlist on Steam (opens in a new tab)

The full game comes out in spring 2027 for Windows, macOS and Linux, and it plays on Steam Deck. When you wishlist it, Steam emails you on launch day and shows Noctiluca to more players.
