Ask the guest book

Before people book, they ring us with the same five questions. Our guests have usually answered them better than we could, in the book on the stove bench in the Stube. When Marta finds such a line, she marks it in pink.

  • Will the children be bored?
  • Can we get there without a car?
  • What if it rains all week?
  • Is it really quiet at night?
  • Is the food worth staying in for?
