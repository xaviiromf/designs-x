The Herrera family farms 4.2 hectares of dry-farmed bush vines in Guarilihue, in the Itata Valley. The Cinsault is ready a week early this year. We need sixty pairs of hands each day, and we feed everyone who comes.

Los Quillayes: Cinsault planted 1958, 23.8 °Brix. We pick it Saturday from 7:30.

    How the weekend goes

    We are three generations and six blocks of bush vines, with no wires and no irrigation. Every bunch is cut by hand and carried out in 18 kg crates. Last March forty-one people came, from Concepción, Chillán, Santiago and two from Lyon, and we picked it all in two days.
