    How the weekend goes

    We are three generations and six blocks of bush vines, with no wires and no irrigation. Last March forty-one people came, from Concepción, Chillán, Santiago and two from Lyon, and we picked it all in two days.

    Saturday 13 March

    1. 7:00Coffee and secateurs at the shed
    2. 7:30Los Quillayes, the 1958 Cinsault
    3. 10:00La Higuera
    4. 12:30We stop before the heat
    5. 13:30Lunch under the fig tree
    6. 17:00Sorting table and the first press, if you want to stay

    Sunday 14 March

    1. 7:30El Alto, the 1890s País
    2. 10:00Don Lalo, then El Bajo
    3. 13:30Lunch with last year's wine
    4. 16:00Lift to the Coelemu bus
