Cheapest: the season pass, € 689. Day passes alone would cost € 806.

Day pass, € 62. 1 to 5 days: buy by the day. Five day passes cost € 310, still less than a week.

Week pass, € 318. 6 to 12 days: a week pass at € 53 a day, plus day passes for the rest. Two weeks come to € 636.

Season pass, € 689. 13 days or more: the season pass is cheaper, even if your days come as two full weeks. Every day after that costs nothing.

Family pass, € 1,590. Two adults and two children pay € 1,512 for 9 days with week and day passes. From the 10th day the family pass, € 1,590, is cheaper, and a third child skis on it free.
