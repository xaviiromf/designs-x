Which Ferntal pass pays for itself

Every pass opens all seven lifts, from the Talbahn gondola to the Kar T-bar, for 41 km of piste between 1,050 and 2,080 m. Pick by how many days you’ll ski.

  • Day passEUR 62One day, 8:30 to 16:30
  • Week passEUR 318Six days in a row
  • Season passEUR 6895 December to 6 April
  • Family passEUR 1,590The season for 2 adults and up to 3 children

Adult prices. Youth 16 to 19 and children 6 to 15 pay less; children under 6 ski free.

How many days will you ski?

Each tick on the cable is one day skied; the cable is the cheapest way to pay for them.

14 days. Cheapest: the season pass, EUR 689. Day passes alone would cost EUR 868.

Day pass EUR 62

1 to 5 days: buy by the day. Five day passes cost EUR 310, still less than a week.

Week pass EUR 318

6 to 12 days: a week pass at EUR 53 a day, plus day passes for the rest. Two weeks come to EUR 636.

Season pass EUR 689

13 days or more: the season pass is cheaper, even if your days come as two full weeks. Every day after that costs nothing.

Family pass EUR 1,590

Two adults and two children pay EUR 1,512 for 9 days with week and day passes. From the 10th day the family pass, EUR 1,590, is cheaper, and a third child skis on it free.
