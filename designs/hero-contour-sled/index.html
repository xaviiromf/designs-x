Rodel, a night sled game by Hangwind

A new mountain every night, one sled, and a line down that nobody else has ridden.

Rodel is a downhill toboggan game played on a contour map. Read the lines, pick a gully and ride it in one go: fast where the lines crowd, slow where they open out. Reach the tarn by dawn, or dig in and try another line.
