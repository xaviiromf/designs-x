    The four rules

    1. Every model has 300 triangles or fewer, counted after triangulation. Characters, cars and the level each count as models.
    2. Textures are 64 by 64 pixels at most, with 16 colours each.
    3. The game renders at 320 by 240 or lower, with no anti-aliasing and no smoothing filters.
    4. 72 hours, alone or in a pair, on the theme announced at the start: Low tide.
