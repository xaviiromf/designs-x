The company of
The Winter’s Tale

Eight actors play seventeen parts between them, the bear included. Five more people get the stage into a village hall by six, light it, run it, and have it back in the van by eleven. This is everyone on the road from 9 September to 28 November: forty-one halls from Cartmel to Kirkby Stephen. In the prompt book each of us is two letters in a circle, so that is how we have drawn ourselves.

Down centre Setting line Prompt corner Row A Sound and light desk

A ground plan of the fit-up stage, seen from above. The eight actors stand in a line across the stage for the curtain call. Nell Garside is in the prompt corner, Gwen Arkwright is at the sound and light desk at the back of the hall, and Oskar Lindqvist, Maggie Doyle and Bea Cottrell sit in Row A. Choose anyone to bring them down centre and open their note. Arrow keys move between people and Escape clears the stage.

Pick anyone to give them their bow.

Cast

  • From Llandeilo; with us since 2021.

    Plays a jealous king until the interval and a pickpocket after it. Keeps the company tea urn.

  • From Chapeltown, Leeds; her first tour.

    Spends Act V standing perfectly still on a beer crate under a dust sheet. Taught chemistry for eleven years before Bristol Old Vic.

  • From Bradford; with us since 2018.

    Also our fight director, which is why the shove in Act I looks like it hurts.

  • From Enniskillen; with us since 2016, our longest-serving actor.

    Opens the second half as Time with an hourglass made from two jam jars.

  • From Barrow-in-Furness; with us since 2022.

    Is pursued by a bear every night at about twenty to nine. Plays fiddle at the sheep-shearing.

  • From Moss Side, Manchester; with us since 2024.

    The bear packs into one laundry bag and takes him ninety seconds to get into. Plays bass in the shearing band.

  • From Workington; her first tour.

    Grows up sixteen years in a fifteen-minute interval. Came to us from our youth company in Whitehaven.

  • From Dumfries; with us since 2025.

    Drives the second shift up the A684 and knows every cattle grid on it.

Stage management

  • From Sedbergh; with us since 2012. Works from the prompt corner.

    Calls every cue, drives the van and holds the book. If the hall has a piano, she has already asked whether we may move it.

  • From Lancaster; with us since 2020. Works from the sound and light desk.

    Rigs twenty-four lanterns on two stands in under an hour and plugs them into whatever the hall has, usually the kitchen.

Creative team

  • From Kirkby Stephen; artistic director since she started the company in 2009.

    Started the company with a borrowed trailer and a two-hander for nine halls. Sits in Row A on the first night, then leaves them to it.

  • Born in Gothenburg; lives in Kendal.

    Designed a set that folds into six road cases and fits through a 760 mm door, the narrowest on the tour, at Garsdale Village Hall.

  • From Hawes.

    Wrote the shearing songs on the harmonium in Hawes Methodist chapel. The cast play all of it live.
