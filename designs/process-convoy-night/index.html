How a convoy night runs

Halfway Station is a flight club of about sixty pilots in Longhaul. Every Friday at 21:00 CET we fly one convoy together, in five steps. Sign up for Friday or say hello on Discord (opens in a new tab).

  1. 1

    Muster

    20:30 CET

    Join the voice channel half an hour early. We check every mic, count the ships and assign the three wings. New pilots fly beside a mentor.

    • pick a lead and agree the gate calls.
    • load up and report tonnage to the lead.
    • take the two flanks and the rear.
  2. 2

    Plot

    20:45 CET

    The lead reads out the route: six jumps and two fuel stops, about ninety minutes in all. Everyone sets the same waypoints before anyone moves.

    • check each gate on the list for traffic.
    • work out fuel for six jumps and a reserve.
    • mark both stops, where the convoy is slow.
  3. 3

    Jump

    21:00 CET

    The wings jump in order, thirty seconds apart. Nobody jumps before the call, and nobody is left on the near side of a gate.

    • jump first and call the far side clear.
    • follow on the call, in one column.
    • jump last and report the convoy complete.
  4. 4

    Dock

    22:30 CET

    At the far station the cargo is sold as one lot and the credits are split evenly. A scout earns exactly what a hauler earns.

    • hold outside and watch the approach.
    • dock two at a time and unload.
    • stay out until the last hauler is in.
  5. 5

    Debrief

    22:45 CET

    Flight logs go up in the club channel with the night's totals. Then everyone votes on next Friday's route from three options.

    • post gate times and any trouble spots.
    • post tonnage delivered and each share.
    • post contacts seen along the lane.

Convoy rules

Six rules. The lead reads them out at every muster, so nobody has to remember them.

  1. Be on voice by 20:30. Late pilots join at the first fuel stop.
  2. Fly the wing you were given. Swap only at a stop, and tell the lead.
  3. Jump on the call, never before it. Thirty seconds between wings.
  4. Nobody is left behind. If a ship misses a jump, two escorts go back for it.
  5. Shares are equal. Scouts and escorts earn what haulers earn.
  6. Keep voice clear during a jump. Chat is for the fuel stops.
