## Why it works

**The one bold idea: the road reads with you.** A sticky rail on the left is a schematic of Ruta 7 drawn as a road, not a transit line: one meandering moss line from the Puerto Montt depot to Villa O'Higgins, lake-coloured dashes where the road is a ferry (La Arena to Puelche, the Hornopirén crossings, Puerto Yungay), a tight zigzag for the Cuesta Queulat switchbacks, Lago General Carrera as a small turquoise shape beside the road, a dotted stub above the depot for "before you leave", and a bar where the road ends. The six FAQ stages are the stops, and their labels are the section nav. The rail answers two questions:

- **Where am I in the trip?** One value, r (stage index plus how far a reading line 30% down the viewport is through that stage), drives everything. The travelled road inks in lenga to r, the stage at r is `aria-current`, and the chilco van chases r along the road (0.7s power3.out, rotated to the road), so it always comes to rest at the ink's leading end. The trip meter is read off the van's own road position, so the drums turn exactly as the van covers road: 0 while you read Booking, 46 to 205 through Ferries, 205 to 900 through Gravel. The van follows rather than scrubs 1:1, which keeps it a drive and not story-cargo-sailing's scrubbed marker.
- **Where does this answer matter?** Each question carries a `data-km` and a short `data-place`. Hovering or focusing it sends a hollow chilco ghost pin to that point with its place lettered beside it ("Coyhaique workshop / km 630"), in the empty lane left of the road, away from the stage labels; the label rolls to the next place with the masked swap. Opening an answer parks a quieter pin (70% scale, no label) on its place for as long as it stays open; the bright pin glides out from it and back. On phones the strip carries the pin and the stage button shows its km while it is out. Every open answer also prints its full place as its first line, so nothing about place is pointer-only.

Further reasons it holds together:

- **A camper's trip meter, not a scoreboard.** Four drums in a recessed moss bezel: paper numerals on lenga drum faces with curvature shading, at 2rem on desktop and 1.25rem on phones, captioned "Trip" and "km". Higher drums only roll while the one below passes 9 (real odometer carry), so 0205 to 0900 reads as a drive, not a count-up. On load the desk resets it: the road draws first, then the drums roll back from the last renter's trip to 0000, and the van appears at the desk. The rail owns the left column from the top of the section, so the whole road and this reset are in the first view. The meter is `aria-hidden`; the `aria-current` stage link and the printed place lines carry the state in text.
- **Colour encodes meaning.** Lake turquoise is water only: ferry legs, Lago General Carrera (an east-west lake with the road on its western shore and the Chile Chico spur along its south shore), italic water labels and in-answer links (they lead across to another answer). Chilco is only ever you: the van, the current stage, the ghost pin, the row sweep, the focus ring. Untravelled road is moss, travelled road is lenga. Italic is lettered only for water, following the cartographic rule.
- **One nav.** The stage labels on the map are `nav[aria-label="Stages of the trip"]`. On phones the same `.rail` becomes a 56px sticky strip: the meter, a straight road with the same stops, ferry dashes, ink, van and ghost pin, and a stage button whose label rolls to the current stage and opens the same links as a sheet. Below 400px the button drops its chevron so the road gets the room; the strip van is 16 by 9 and the pin 12px.
- **Quiet everywhere else.** The H2 is large but unaccented; questions are plain rows on real rules, present from first paint (the only line that draws on scroll is the road); one 62ch answer column; no cards, no eyebrows, no search, no pointer follower. The plus sits in a 28px ring that fills lenga on hover or focus; its vertical arm turns flat into a minus when open. An open answer gets a paper band with a 6px radius; buttons are pills; the sheet has a 14px radius. Radius follows hierarchy.
- **Accessible structure.** `section[aria-labelledby]` with an h2; six stage `section`s with h3s (`tabindex="-1"`, ids `stage-booking` to `stage-south`); every question is `h4 > button[aria-expanded][aria-controls]`; panels are plain containers (no `role=region`: 22 landmarks would bury the page's landmark list) and `inert` when closed. The questions ship as plain heading text and the script upgrades them to buttons, so without the libraries there is no button that does nothing. Open all / Close all carries its state in its label only (no `aria-expanded`), so it is announced once. Rows are independent and Open all exists, because readers compare answers. The map SVGs are `aria-hidden` with a visually hidden sentence ("Schematic of Ruta 7 from Puerto Montt to Villa O'Higgins, not to scale."), and the rail says "Ruta 7, not to scale".
- **No dead ends.** Stage links (which set the rail directly and hold it until you scroll, so a short stage or one at the foot of the page is still the one marked), five in-answer cross-links ("the Argentina permit" twice, "return checklist", "message the desk", "the deposit") and `#q-...` hashes glide to their target, open the question, move focus and sweep the row once to show where you landed. "Email the desk" is a `mailto:` on the reserved `.example` domain with `target="_blank" rel="noopener"`. "Copy address" writes to the clipboard and says "Copied", or selects the address and says "Press Ctrl+C" (Cmd+C on Apple) where the clipboard is blocked. No phone or WhatsApp link, because a fictional number could reach a real person.
- **Works without the libraries.** If the CDN fails or JavaScript is off, `html.js` is never set: every answer is open under a plain heading, the icons, meter, map and toggle are hidden, and the stages are a wrapped list of plain anchors.
- **Palette checked.** Lichen `#DCDECC` is the grey-green of lichen on roadside rock under an Aysén overcast, not cream (default 1), and sits at least ΔE 6 from the nearest grounds in the collection (8.7 from testimonials-surf-school, 9.2 from team-research-lab, 10.0 from team-rowing-club, 13.4 from faq-pharmacy, 6.4 from story-glacier-research). The accent is fuchsia-crimson chilco, not terracotta or vermilion. Contrast on lichen: lenga 10.8:1, moss 5.2:1, chilco 5.0:1, lake 4.6:1. On the paper band: lenga 13.7:1, moss 6.6:1, chilco 6.4:1, lake 5.9:1. Paper on lenga 13.7:1. Lake passes AA at any size, so water labels sit at 15px like the stage names; they never outrank the navigation.
- **Reviewed and revised.** The first draft drew stations on a coloured line (too close to hero-transit-line), then scrubbed the van and a per-frame km readout with scroll (the same device as story-cargo-sailing). Then the van and meter moved only on stage changes while the ink scrubbed, which gave three disagreeing "where am I" readings and a meter that never rolled through a stage; now one reading position drives ink, stage and van, the van chases it, and the meter reads the van. Cut along the way: a site-wide cursor disc and then a labelled pointer pill (both repeated the ghost pin), a lens revealing paved and gravel surfaces, a scramble on the meter, a search bar with a status tally (faq-moving-company's signature), and, as the last accessory, the van's side window: the marker is a chilco body with a windscreen that shows which way it faces.

## Do not touch

- **One reading position, one van.** `update()` computes r once; ink reads r, the current stage is `floor(r)`, the van chases r through `driveTo()`, and `drawVan()` sets the meter from the van's position. Never give the meter its own tween, and do not scrub the van 1:1 with scroll. Reduced motion rests all of them on the current stage's stop.
- **One road model.** Places are stored as a stop coordinate (stop index plus fraction) and converted to km, path fraction and strip position. The van and pin read a 240-point lookup table sampled once from `getPointAtLength` (viewBox units do not change with layout); positions and rotation are interpolated from it, never measured per frame. Stop km: depot 0, La Arena 46, Chaitén 205, El Maitén 900, Cochrane 970, Villa O'Higgins 1,240.
- **Odometer carry.** `setKm` turns the units drum continuously and moves each higher drum only by the fraction the drum below spends between 9 and 10. Keep the `#selftest` checks (`runSelfTest`) when you edit it.
- **Stage links win.** `land()` sets `lock` to the target stage; scroll-derived r is ignored until the reader wheels, swipes or moves the page by any other means (`lockY`). Keep it: short stages and the last stages cannot reach the reading line.
- **Water is lake, you are chilco.** Do not use lake for anything that is not water or a cross-link, or chilco for anything that is not the reader, the van, the current stage or focus.
- **Italic only for water labels.** Every other label is upright Asap at wdth 87.5.
- **Disclosure semantics.** Headings upgraded to buttons by the script, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `inert` on closed panels, real-height `grid-template-rows` (no measuring), close faster than open, and focus moved by every anchor.
- **Map proportions.** The map box keeps the 240 by 900 viewBox aspect; stop and label positions are percentages of that box, set from the same coordinates the road uses (`data-stops`). Change the road and the stops together.

## Adapting

1. **Another route.** Redraw `#road-map` in the 240 by 900 viewBox, keep the left `--lane` free for the pin label, keep `pathLength="1"`, and list the seven stop coordinates in `data-stops` (the stub start, the six stages' stops and the end of the road). Update `STOP_KM` in the script, the ferry or water `g.m-ferry` segments, the lake path, the map labels (`--x`, `--y` in viewBox units) and the strip's `data-sx` ferry positions (percent of the strip). Five to seven stages fit; the strip spaces stages evenly, so it needs no real distances.
2. **Questions.** Give each question a `data-km`, a short `data-place` for the pin label (about 20 characters) and a full place line (`.ans-place`) that names a real point. Questions in the first group (before you leave) always sit on the stub. Keep answers at 50 to 110 words; the band and the 62ch column are built for that.
3. **Groups.** Each group needs a `data-km-start` (the meter's reading for that stage), a `data-km-end`, an h3 id that matches its stage link, and a sentence-case place line. Each stage link needs a `data-short` label for the phone button; the button reserves the width of every short label.
4. **Palette.** Change the six colour tokens; keep one colour for water, one for "you", and an untravelled/travelled pair with at least 3:1 against the ground.
5. **Facts.** The km positions along Ruta 7 and the ferry durations are approximate and written with "about"; the company, prices, deposit, permit and policies are fictional. Verify before real use. The email uses the reserved `.example` domain on purpose.

## Motion

Lenis runs on GSAP's ticker (`autoRaf: false`, `lagSmoothing(0)`) and feeds `ScrollTrigger.update`, inside a `gsap.matchMedia` branch for `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` whose cleanup removes the tick, restores `lagSmoothing(500, 33)` and destroys Lenis. The same branch, in both modes, creates the one ScrollTrigger (start 0, end max) that feeds the reading position. A second `matchMedia` branch on the 992px breakpoint drops any sheet state when the nav moves between map and sheet. The disclosures, reading position, van, meter, pin and anchors are bound once outside those branches because they work in both motion modes; they read `prefers-reduced-motion` and `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)` at event time. Hidden starting states exist only under `html.js` and only for what the load timeline animates; questions are readable from first paint. Lenis is stopped while the phone sheet is open. Only transform, opacity, clip-path, stroke dashes and the catalogue's `grid-template-rows` accordion animate; the native cursor is never hidden. Technique ids refer to `research/motion-techniques.md`.

Touch keeps native scrolling (Lenis `syncTouch` off). Every hover has a keyboard and touch equivalent: focus-visible shows the row sweep and moves the ghost pin, a tap opens the answer (which moves the pin), buttons fill on `:active`, and every group heading and open answer prints its place in text.

**Retiming or removing.** CSS-driven timings are the `--dur-*` and `--ease-*` tokens. The load timeline lives in `intro()` (`T0` and `DRAW` for the road, the title at .15, the meter at .8, the van at 1.1). The van drive and chase are `driveTo()`; the meter follows from `drawVan()`; the reading position is `readR()` and `update()`; the ghost pin is `renderGhost()`. To remove the load sequence, delete the `intro()` call (the page then starts in its final state). To make the van jump but keep it, give `driveTo()` its reduced-motion branch unconditionally. To stop the ink scrub, make `update()` use `cur` instead of `r` in both modes; the ink, van and meter then rest on the current stop. Without the CDN the page falls back to every answer open.
