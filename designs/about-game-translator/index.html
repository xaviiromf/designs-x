SourceReading the source

2010 to 2014

I studied Japanese in Bonn and spent a year in Sendai, reading game scripts to learn how people really talk: shop talk, threats, jokes. My first paid work was checking a farming game for a small German publisher, line by line, against the build.

RoughFast and literal

2014 to 2017

Three years in-house at a localisation agency in Hamburg. I wrote rough drafts for 11 games, about 1.1 million words, and learned that a rough draft is for the editor, never for the player.

EditOther people's lines

2017 to 2020

I moved to editing other translators' scripts for meaning, voice and consistency. I wrote the agency's first style sheet, and I started sending back lines that were correct but dead.

Fit to boxTwenty-two characters

2020 to 2023

Freelance from Düsseldorf. My first job with a hard limit was a handheld role-playing game with three rows of 22 characters. I built the overlay I still use, and cut 4,000 lines to fit without shrinking the font.

In the gameLines people play

2023 to now

Credited on 14 shipped games, working directly with studios in Japan. I play every scene in the build before I sign it off, because a line can read well in a spreadsheet and still break in the box.

    My working terms

    Editing
    52 EUR an hour, about 2,500 words of German or English an hour.
