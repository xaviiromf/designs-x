    Molo 4 is a hackerspace run by its members, in the old pilot station on Pier 4 in Trieste. Open night is every Tuesday from 19:00: walk in, no booking. The door is on the seaward side, past the bollards.

    Find the door

    Chart at 100 m

    The pilot station is the last building on the pier. Cars stop at the row of five bollards; people and bikes carry on. Go round the building by its south side. The door faces the open sea, under a red lamp.

    Address
    Stazione Piloti, Molo 4, 34135 Trieste
    Open night
    Tuesdays from 19:00 until the last member leaves
    Tram
    Line 6 to Rive Molo 4, then 450 m on foot
    Bikes
    Six hoops on the station wall, a long rack at the foot of the pier
    Cars
    Silos car park, 875 m away. Nothing drives past the bollards.
    Step-free
    Yes. The quay is level all the way and the door is 90 cm wide.

    How to find the door

    From the tram stop Rive Molo 4, line 6. It is 450 m, about six minutes, level all the way.

    1. 1Cross the waterfront road toward the sea.25 m
    2. 2Turn right and follow the quay to the foot of Pier 4. The long bike rack is on your left.95 m
    3. 3Turn left onto the pier and walk out along the crane rails to the row of five bollards.275 m
    4. 4Pass between the bollards and go round the pilot station by its left side, the south one.35 m
    5. 5The door is in the seaward wall, under the red lamp. Ring the bell marked M4.20 m

    If the red lamp is dark, nobody is in. Ask in the Matrix room before you walk out.
