Every March since 1986, we walk to the end of Tarrant Glacier and mark where the ice stops.

March 20261,870 m behind cairn T3

1986 The first line

The front sits against cairn T3, 410 m above the sea. We built the cairn so every later survey starts from the same rock.

1993 The tongue thins

The front is 180 m behind the cairn. Stake S2, drilled 12 m into the ice in 1991, now stands 9 m clear of the surface.

1999 A lake opens

Meltwater ponds behind the 1986 moraine. We map it at 0.12 km² and start sounding it from a packraft.

2008 The front floats

The ice now ends in water 40 m deep. It lifts off its bed and retreats 93 m this year, the most we have measured.

2016 Calving season

An 80 m slab breaks off on 9 March, the day before the survey. We measure the new front from the cairn by laser.

2026 The forty-first line

The front is 1,870 m behind cairn T3. The lake covers 2.3 km² and is 64 m deep where the ice stood in 2008.
