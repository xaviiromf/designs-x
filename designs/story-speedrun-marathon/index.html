The estimate

Every run on the schedule is booked by its estimate. mossgiant asked for 11:40 for Lanternfall any%, and the next runner starts setting up the moment that slot ends. Go over, and the overrun comes out of someone else's time.

The splits

The timer cuts the run into four splits and compares each one with the runner's personal best, the lilac ghost on the stage. Red is behind that pace, green is ahead. Gold is rarer: the fastest this runner has ever played that segment, on any day.

The skip

Past the cistern the route climbs the bell stair. Unless you jump into the corner under the ledge on one of two frames, a thirtieth of a second, and come out on the far side of the wall. Hit it and save 38 seconds. Miss it, drop back, take the stairs anyway and lose 12.

The incentive

Donations decide how a run is played. At 5,000 kr the last room is played blindfolded, and the chat got there with three minutes to spare. From the door on, mossgiant counts steps and listens for the lanterns.

Time

11:31.2. A personal best by 5.7 seconds and 8.8 under the estimate, with the last room played blind. While the run was on screen it raised 18,460 kr for the children's hospital fund, and the next runner started on schedule.
