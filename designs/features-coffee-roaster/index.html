  • Two coffees, 250 g each

    One familiar, one a stretch. October brings Finca El Mirador, a washed Caturra from Huila with red apple and panela. It also brings Kebel Aricha, a natural from Yirgacheffe that tastes of blueberry and black tea.

  • Roasted Tuesday, posted Wednesday

    We roast subscriptions on the first Tuesday of the month in 12 kg batches and post them the next morning. The roast date is printed on every bag. Let it rest a week: it tastes best from day 7 to day 35.

  • A lot card with the price we paid

    Farm, producer, altitude, process, harvest, and what we paid: $7.40 a kilo, free on board, to Luz Dary Ortiz’s growers’ association for El Mirador. The curve on the back of the card is the roast log above.

  • A brew card for each lot

    Every lot comes with the recipe we settled on at the roastery bar. Whole beans, or ground for filter, espresso, cafetière or moka.
