Visit

Getting here

On Nabrzeże Pomorskie, fifteen minutes’ walk from Gdynia Główna station down Świętojańska. The waterfront car park is free after 18:00.

Seating and views

1,480 seats on three levels. The choir seats behind the platform face the conductor and cost 39 zł, except at choral concerts, when the chorus sits in them.

Step-free access

Step-free from the pier entrance; two lifts to the circle; 12 wheelchair spaces; hearing loop in the stalls; a companion seat is free.

Bar and cloakroom

The foyer bar opens 90 minutes before each concert. Order interval drinks before you sit down. Cloakroom free.

Box office and hours

Open 10:00 to 18:00, Monday to Saturday, and until the interval on concert nights.

Under-26s pay 20 zł for any seat; bring ID when you collect.

The hall

Sala Nabrzeże opened on the waterfront in 1926, the year Gdynia became a town: a shoebox, long, tall and narrow, with 1,480 seats on three levels facing the platform. The choir seats rise behind it, under the 1926 Biernacki organ, restored in 2019.

Full, the hall rings for 2.1 seconds, which is why a quartet sounds close and the Mahler sounds enormous from the same seat.

Learning

Family concerts are on Sundays at 11:00 and last under an hour; children sit free with an adult. Schools concerts run on weekday mornings in term and are free for Gdynia schools.

The Gdynia Youth Orchestra rehearses on the platform on Saturday mornings. Anyone can sit in the circle and listen.

Support

Friends of Sala Nabrzeże pay 240 zł a year and book two weeks before general sale. The money paid for the organ’s restoration in 2019 and pays for the schools concerts now.
