Visit

Getting here

Harbour Quay, eight minutes’ walk from Port Ardley station. Buses 4 and 11 stop at Quay Gate. The Quay car park is free after 18:00.

Seating and views

1,480 seats on three levels. The choir seats behind the platform face the conductor and cost £9, except at choral concerts, when the chorus sits in them.

Step-free access

Step-free from the Quay entrance; two lifts to the circle; 12 wheelchair spaces; hearing loop in the stalls; a companion seat is free.

Bar and cloakroom

The foyer bar opens 90 minutes before each concert. Order interval drinks before you sit down. Cloakroom free.

Box office and hours

Open 10:00 to 18:00, Monday to Saturday, and until the interval on concert nights.

The hall

Kestle Hall opened on Harbour Quay in 1926: a shoebox, long, tall and narrow, with 1,480 seats on three levels facing the platform. The choir seats rise behind it, under the 1926 Harrison organ, restored in 2019.

Full, the hall rings for 2.1 seconds, which is why a quartet sounds close and the Mahler sounds enormous from the same seat.

Learning

Family concerts are on Sundays at 11:00 and last under an hour; children sit free with an adult. Schools concerts run on weekday mornings in term and are free for Port Ardley schools.

The Ardley Youth Orchestra rehearses on the platform on Saturday mornings. Anyone can sit in the circle and listen.

Support

Friends of Kestle Hall pay £60 a year and book two weeks before general sale. The money paid for the organ’s restoration in 2019 and pays for the schools concerts now.
