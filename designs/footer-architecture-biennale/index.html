Visit the biennale

22 May to 21 November 2027 in Trieste’s old port. Five venues, two of them underground. One ticket opens all five.

Venues

±0.00 street level

Molo IV shed +1.80

Talks every Thursday and Saturday at 18:00; the book fair fills the east bay.

Open Thursday to Sunday, 10:00 to 20:00.

Magazzino 4 +0.60

Thirty-one practices on foundations, soil and repair, on three floors of an 1891 bonded warehouse.

Open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 19:00. Last entry 18:15.

Old Customs Hall ±0.00

Visitor centre, ticket desk, cloakroom and the press office.

Open Tuesday to Sunday, 9:30 to 19:30.

Salt Cistern −6.40

Three installations in the vaulted reservoir that held the port’s fresh water until 1958.

Timed entry every 20 minutes, Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 18:00. It is 14 °C inside; bring a layer.

Rail tunnel 1912 −14.20

A 400 m sound walk through the disused harbour rail tunnel.

Timed entry every 15 minutes, Friday to Sunday, 11:00 to 18:00. 13 °C and damp underfoot; wear sturdy shoes.
