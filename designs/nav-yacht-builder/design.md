## Why it works

**The bold idea: the duck travels along the batten.** A loftsman fairs a hull's lines on the floor with a long spruce batten held in place by lead weights called ducks. Here the header's lower edge is that batten, drawn as one SVG path, and a small lead duck sits on it under the link for the section in view. When the section changes, or a link is hovered or focused, the duck slides to the new link. As it moves, the batten flexes into a fair curve under its weight (up to 4px, in proportion to the distance), a blue "faired" segment resizes to the new link's width, and everything settles straight in 0.55s expo.out with no overshoot, because a spruce batten is stiff and is not a string. At the top of the page the duck parks under the logo, and at the commission form it parks under the "Commission a boat" button.

- **One object, three forms.** The full-width bar scrubs into a centred pill over the first 200px and the batten goes with it, clipped inside the pill. The phone bar has no links, so the batten carries six pencil station ticks, one per section from the logo to the Menu button, and the duck parks at the current one (its tick turns ink), which reads as how far down the page you are. On phones the pill grows into the menu panel, and the batten turns vertical down the panel's left edge, carrying the duck to the current, hovered or focused row. There is no second idea competing with it. The hero's lines plan is static, section headings do not animate, and the only scroll-drawn rules are the five build stages.
- **The flex is visible.** The travel runs on one linear clock: the duck's position follows expo.out, but the sag follows a slower cubic inside a sine, so the bend peaks around 0.1s while the duck is still decelerating and relaxes with zero slope at 0.55s. With both on the same expo curve, the sag was over before the eye could see it.
- **The reader sees every move.** Below the hero the header hides on downward scroll. A section change while it is hidden does not move the duck off screen. The move waits, and plays 0.18s after the bar comes back down. During a link glide the header stays down, so a click always shows the duck travelling.
- **The batten answers the hand, a little.** With a mouse, the batten bows toward the pointer while it is in the header band but not over a link: up when the pointer is above the line, down below it, never more than 3px, and more as the pointer nears the line. Over a link the duck owns the batten, so the bow relaxes and the duck settles on a straight line. The pointer's bow and the duck's sag are combined into one fair quadratic (sags added, control points weighted by sag), so the duck still rides the curve. It relaxes straight in 0.6s expo.out when the pointer leaves.
- **Bending without changing the path.** The batten path is written once, as a quadratic from (0,0) to (L,0) with its control point at (L/2, 1). Applying `matrix(1, 0, cx - L/2, s, 0, 8)` keeps both ends fixed and moves the control point to (cx, s). That is an exact affine image of the bent curve, so only the group's transform changes per frame, with `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke` keeping the stroke 3px wide. The control point goes to `2 × duck - L/2` (clamped to the batten) so the deepest point sits under the duck, and the duck rides the curve by solving for the curve's height at its own x. The self-check at `#selftest` verifies both against a sampled quadratic.
- **Colour is the floor.** `--floor` is the pale, cool paint of a loft floor, and `--sheet` is a slightly lighter surface for the pill, panel and fields, edged with a pencil rule. Pencil graphite carries text, the duck is cast lead (graphite mixed 70% into `--lead`, with a thin sheet-coloured highlight along its back, at 52 × 26px so it reads against the grey labels), the batten is spruce, and the only accent is a lofting blue (`--ink`). It is used only on things you can act on or that are current: the faired segment, focus rings, the buttons, the chosen boat. No navy-and-white stripes, rope or chart vocabulary.
- **Structure is information.** Boat specs are a labelled `dl` (length overall, beam, draught, displacement, berths, build time, price). Name and role sit on separate lines. The sail plans are drawn to one scale, so the Hope 52 is visibly bigger than the 34. The build stages are an ordered list whose markers are durations ("About 3 weeks", "5 to 9 months"), because they are a real sequence. The durations are for a Hope 34 (about 11 months in all), and the intro says the bigger boats take longer, mostly in fit-out, so they agree with the spec rows. There are no eyebrows, no 01/02 numbers and no arrows on links.
- **Behaviour does not depend on motion.** The section spy, menu, anchors, hide-on-scroll and form all live outside `gsap.matchMedia`, so under reduced motion or with the CDN blocked everything still works, with instant changes. The motion branch only adds Lenis, tweens, the pointer bow on the batten and the entry side of the button fill.
- **Copy from the customer's side.** Three real boats with full specs and prices, five strip-planking stages with materials and durations, "shoes come off at the door" because the loft floor is painted, and a result that says nothing was sent.
- **The hero earns the fold.** At 1440 × 1000 the lines plan's profile (sheer, waterline and keel) sits above the fold, the only drawing that echoes the batten. On phones the plan is cropped to its bow half, stations 6 to 0, with the labels hidden rather than shrunk to 6px.

## Do not touch

- **Two header layers.** The outer `.hdr` takes the hide translate (`translateY(-120%)`, 0.4s expo.out). The inner `.bar` takes the pill: `.bar__bg::before` is clipped with `inset()` plus `round`, the logo and `.bar__end` translate by `--p × --shift`, and the batten is clipped by `--p × (--ins + --batten-pad)`. Putting both on one element makes the scrubbed pill and the hide transition fight.
- **The pill geometry.** `--ins` (side inset, `max(8px, (width - 880px) / 2)` from 992px, 8px below) and `--shift` (how far the logo and buttons travel inward) are measured on resize. The duck's targets are cached in layout space with a sign: `+1` for the logo, `-1` for links and buttons. Its x is `b + g × p × shift`, so it stays under its link while the pill forms. Do not read rects per frame.
- **The batten maths.** The path data is written only on resize. Per frame, only the `transform` of the two `<g>` groups, the ink segment's `clip-path` and the duck's `transform` change. The control sag `s` is twice the visible sag. `matrixFor` never lets `s` reach 0, because a singular matrix stops non-scaling strokes rendering.
- **The settle.** Travel is one linear proxy tween `u` from 0 to 1. Position and segment width use `e = expoOut(u)`. The sag is `from × (1 - e) + max × sin(π v)` with `v = 1 - (1 - u)³`, so it is continuous when interrupted, peaks while the duck decelerates and ends exactly at 0. Do not drive the sag from `e`, which hides the bend, and do not add elastic or back eases.
- **Deferred travel.** `setCurrent()` only marks `pending` while the header has `is-hidden`, and `show()` plays the move 0.18s after the class comes off. The header does not hide while a link glide holds the spy `lock`.
- **The duck waits for the font.** `#duck` starts with `visibility: hidden` in the markup and is shown after `document.fonts.ready` and a re-measure, and the load timeline plays only then, so the duck never jumps 2px after first paint.
- **Spy band and section heights.** IntersectionObserver with `rootMargin: -40% 0px -55% 0px`. Every section needs `min-height: 50svh` (more than the band's 45%), or clicking a short section's link will light the next one.
- **Menu rules.** The toggle has `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. The panel is `inert` when closed and comes after the toggle in the DOM, so the tab order is logo, Close, panel links. Focus moves to the current section's link (or the first) when it opens, and a preview ignores focus that is not `:focus-visible`, so the duck opens on the current row. Escape, a click outside, a link click or focus leaving the header closes it, and Escape and outside clicks return focus to the toggle. The keyboard is never trapped. Lenis stops while it is open. The header never hides with the menu open, with focus inside it or with the mouse in the top 72px.
- **Hover only on fine pointers.** Every `:hover` rule sits in `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, so a tapped button does not stay filled. Touch gets a brief `:active` fill and the 0.97 press; keyboard gets `:focus-visible`. Fields take a 2px ink outline at 2px offset on focus, as links and buttons do.
- **Real form controls.** The boat choices are real radios in a fieldset. Errors appear under the field with `aria-describedby` (and `aria-invalid` on the inputs), and focus goes to the first invalid field. The result heading gets focus and is scrolled into view.
- **Contrast.** Graphite on floor is 12.6:1, lead on floor 5.4:1, ink on floor 7.3:1, sheet on ink 8.0:1, fault on floor 7.4:1, field edges 3.8:1 on sheet. Spruce is decorative only (2.1:1) and must never carry text.

## Motion

Ease family: expo.out for travel and arrivals, expo.inOut for draws and morphs, none for anything scrubbed. Leaving runs faster than entering. Everything runs inside one `gsap.matchMedia` (`motion`, `reduce`, `fine`). Lenis (lerp 0.1, `autoRaf: false`, `anchors: false`) runs on `gsap.ticker` with `lagSmoothing(0)`. Cleanup reverts the menu timeline, removes the pointer listeners and the ticker callback, destroys Lenis, straightens both battens, and switches the header to the instant compact state. Technique ids refer to `research/motion-techniques.md`.

## Adapting

1. **Sections and links.** Keep the `ORDER` array, the section ids, the `data-spy` and `data-duck` attributes on the bar links, the panel links and the CTAs, and a `tabindex="-1"` heading in each section in step. `top` parks the duck on the logo and the last id parks it on the CTA. Four links fit the 880px pill comfortably. A fifth needs the 880 in `measure()` raised.
2. **Tokens.** Keep the roles: one painted ground, one lighter surface, a text colour, a secondary text at 4.5:1 or better on both ground and surface, a decorative line colour for the batten, and one accent for action and current state. The accent must pass 4.5:1 against the ground and the surface must pass 4.5:1 on the accent, because button labels flip to it on hover.
3. **Another tool on the line.** The rig takes any small SVG in place of the duck. It is drawn on a 44 × 22 grid with its base on y=21 and shown at 52 × 26px. If you change the size, change the `26.3px` top offset in `.duck`, the `-4.7px` left of `.duck--v`, and the `pos - 26` / `pos - 13` translations in `render()` (half the width and half the height). Keep about 18px clear between the row's buttons and the batten (`--batten-y` minus `--row-h`). A bookmark ribbon, a slide-rule cursor or a tide-gauge float all work. Keep the stiff settle unless the material really is elastic.
4. **Header geometry.** `--bar-h`, `--row-h`, `--batten-y`, `--pill-top`, `--pill-r` (half of bar height minus top inset), `--pill-pad` and `--batten-pad` are set for narrow screens and again from 992px. Change them together. The duck needs about 18px clear between the row's buttons and the batten.
5. **Retiming or removing motion.** CSS durations and curves are the `--dur-*` and `--ease-*` tokens. The duck's travel is `rig.go(id, seconds)`, with 0.55 for the spy and 0.45 for previews. The sag cap is the `Math.min(4, dx / 60)` in `go()`, and setting it to 0 gives a straight-sliding indicator. The phone station ticks are the six `.tick` spans in `.batten__in`, one per `ORDER` id. The pointer bow is the `-3 * ...` in the fine branch's `bend`: lower the 3 to stiffen the batten, or delete the `if (fine)` block (and its cleanup line) to remove it. The load rises are the two `.from()` lines after the batten draw in the first timeline. To remove the pill, delete the ScrollTrigger in the motion branch and the `if (!M.on)` line in the scroll listener, and set `--p: 0`. To remove hide-on-scroll, delete the `is-hidden` line in the scroll listener. To remove the load, delete the first timeline. To drop the libraries, remove the three CDN tags: the page then behaves like the reduced-motion version, with every state change instant.
6. **Stack translation.** Keep one current-section source (the observer), one rig object per batten, the two header layers, and the menu's inert, focus and Escape rules. In a framework, measure targets in a resize effect and call `render()` from the scroll handler, never from layout reads per frame.
