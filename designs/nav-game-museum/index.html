1

Arcade hall

Sixty-four cabinets stand in the old engine shed, from wood-sided uprights of 1978 to four-player machines from the late nineties. Every one is on free play: your ticket is the coin. When a cabinet breaks, it crosses the corridor to the Workshop and a card on the empty spot says when it will be back.

Cabinets
64, and 3 pinball tables
Noise
Loud. Ear defenders are free at the desk.
Desk
Booked tickets are collected and paid here
Walk on to the Console gallery

2

Console gallery

One home console for every year from 1976 to 2016, forty-one in all, each wired to a television sold the same year. Sit at any station and play. The games in the gallery change on the first Sunday of every month, and the list for next month is pinned by the door.

Stations
41, two players at each
New games
First Sunday of the month
Walk on to the Handheld corridor

3

Handheld corridor

The depot's inspection pit, 52 metres long, is now lined with 230 handheld and pocket LCD games under glass. Twelve hang on short chains so you can play them. The plaques beside the rest say who made them and what they cost new.

Length
52 m, step-free from the corridor
Handhelds
230, of which 12 are playable
Walk on to the Workshop

4

Workshop

Two technicians repair circuit boards, screens and controllers behind a glass wall, and you can watch from the bench. On the last Saturday of each month, bring a broken controller and they will show you how to open it and fix it.

Watch
Wednesday to Sunday, 14:00 to 17:00
Repair day
Last Saturday of the month, 10:00 to 12:00, free with a ticket
Walk on to the Reading room

5

Reading room

1,900 magazines and 600 manuals, maps and posters, shelved by year on the depot's old timetable racks. Read anything at the ten tables; nothing leaves the room. Please keep your voice down: the arcade is loud enough for the whole building.

Shelves
1,900 magazines, 600 manuals and maps
Seats
10, no booking
Walk on to the Café and shop

6

Café and shop

Coffee, crêpes and one lunch plate a day at tables made from old tram seats. The shop sells repair kits, posters by Lyon printmakers and our own guide to the collection for €14. You can use both without a ticket.

Café
10:00 to 18:30, lunch 11:30 to 14:30
Ticket
Not needed for the café or the shop
Back to the way in

The doors close at 19:00, at 22:00 on Thursdays, and the last entry is an hour before. Questions about access, prams or wheelchairs go to the desk.
