Watch the Six from the middle of the track.

Sixteen teams of two ride the Madison over six days on a 250 m pine track at Hebble Bridge Velodrome, 10 to 15 November. Stand in the track centre, sit at the rail, or see it all from the tier.

How the racing is scored

Each team rides as a relay. One rider races while the other rolls high on the banking, then a hand-sling swaps them.

Gaining a lap on the field beats any number of points. Sprints every 10 laps score 5, 3, 2 and 1, doubled on the last. Teams are ranked by laps, then points.
