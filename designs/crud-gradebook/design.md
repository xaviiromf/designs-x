## Why it works

**The bold idea: mark propagation.** When a teacher commits a mark (Enter, an arrow key, Tab or leaving the cell with a changed, valid value), the numeral is written into the cell: over 0.3s it is revealed left to right like a nib stroke while its colour runs from marker black to green pen. At 0.12s, once the cause is on screen, a faint purple wash spreads outward along that student's row and that assessment's column, cell by cell in order of distance (TR3, 0.07s per cell). When the wash reaches the row's Average cell, the average rolls from the old value to the new one (SC13) and a purple delta chip appears beside it. One step later the wash reaches the Working at cell, and if the grade changed its numeral rolls one digit (7 leaves by the top, 8 arrives from below). When the wash reaches the column's Class mean cell, the mean rolls too. Last, the class average in the hero sentence, the Class mean row and the app bar rolls, and the student's dot slides along the hero's class strip (purple only while it moves). While a chain runs down a column, the column band of the reading ruler steps aside so the wash is never muddied by it. The chain takes about 1.2s and shows the dependency: this mark changed this student's average, grade and this assessment's mean, and nothing else. At 390px the same wash runs down the student's open marks list and up to the average in the row head, and the grade in the details line rolls. Under reduced motion every figure updates at once, the mark is green at once, and the chip still appears.

The demo edit is Tolu Adeyemi, Atomic structure test, 21 to 24. Every figure is computed from the seeded marks (9/12 weight 1, 14/20 weight 1.5, 21/30 weight 2, 41/60 weight 3; the homework does not count). Tolu's average goes from 70.0% to 72.7% (up 2.7, grade 7 to grade 8). The Atomic structure test mean goes from 62.5% to 63.8%, and the class average from 63.1% to 63.4%. The live region then reads: "Tolu Adeyemi, Atomic structure test: 24 out of 30. Average now 72.7%, up 2.7. Now working at grade 8. Atomic structure test mean now 63.8%. Class average 63.4%." (the class average is read only when it changed, so the chain's last figure is heard too). The self-test asserts every one of these values and that exact string.

- **The colours are the marking policy.** UK schools mark in green pen, and students answer feedback in the "purple pen of progress". Green (#0B7A4B) is the teacher's ink: the mark just entered, the primary buttons and the focus ring. Purple (#6B3FA0) appears only for what has just changed: the wash, the delta chip and the pulse on a row that has just been added or restored. Whiteboard red marker (#C7362B) is kept for errors, delete and below-target grades, and below target is always written out ("5, below target 6" in the grid, "below target 6" in the list), never shown by colour alone. Text is dry-erase black marker (#262A33) on cool whiteboard melamine (#F7F9F9). Every rule is aluminium trim (#7A848B), never green. The black marker and the brighter red keep this design apart from crud-ledger's blue-black ink and the #B4 reds of the other crud designs.
- **One type family, chosen for the marker.** Whiteboard markers have a round bullet nib, so every stroke ends round. M PLUS Rounded 1c has the same round terminals, so "9X1 Science" reads like the class name written on the board, without a handwriting face. The class name and section headings are set at 800 (tracking -0.01em, leading 1.0), marks and averages at 700, buttons and the angled titles at 500, body at 400 with 1.6 leading. The face's figures are already one width: measured at 40px, '1111' and '0000' are both 102.4px with or without `tabular-nums`, so columns of marks line up and rolling figures never jiggle the hero sentence. The 13px angled titles render cleanly, so the Zen Maru Gothic fallback was not needed. The face has no italic, so nothing is set in italic.
- **Paper mark book headings.** Assessment titles are written at -60 degrees above narrow mark columns, as teachers write them in a paper mark book, so long titles fit over 4rem columns. The hit target is the upright cell under each title, 44px tall and the full column width, showing "/30" (and "*" when the assessment does not count). The rotated title is only its picture, and it is `pointer-events: none`, so neighbouring titles never steal clicks. The cell opens that assessment in the list below and moves focus to it.
- **The grid scrolls inside its own region, never the page.** The grid sits in a `role="region"` container labelled "Marks for 9X1", with `data-lenis-prevent-horizontal` so sideways wheel and trackpad gestures reach it. It becomes a tab stop only when it actually overflows. The student-name column is sticky, gains a board-coloured ground and a trim edge once scrolled, and `scroll-padding-left` keeps a focused mark from hiding under it. With five assessments the grid fits from about 950px up; with eight it scrolls by about 140px at 1024px.
- **The reading ruler (P1).** On a fine pointer, a translucent aluminium bar one row tall slides behind the hovered row (CSS transform, 0.28s, power3.out), and a faint band marks the hovered column. The row's name and the column's title turn green-pen. The native cursor is untouched. The ruler also follows keyboard focus into any name or cell, parks on a tapped row on touch, and runs on the assessments list too. It teaches the row-and-column reading that the signature then uses.
- **Numbers are computed, never typed.** Every mark is seeded in `seed()`. The hero sentence, averages, means, grades, strip plots and the below-target count all come from it. The weighted average is the sum of (mark / out of x weight) over counted, marked assessments, divided by the sum of their weights. A blank mark means "not handed in" and is left out, while a 0 counts. The class average is the mean of the students' averages. Grades come from the boundary table (9 from 80% down to 1 from 10%, U below), applied to the figure as shown (rounded to one decimal place). So Kwame Boateng's 64.0% is a 7, even though the floating-point value is 63.999. `TODAY` is fixed at 2026-10-22 so the seeds and the self-test are deterministic.
- **The board, framed.** The grid is the whiteboard itself: a 6px aluminium bezel built from trim tints (lit top left, darker bottom right) inside the 14px radius, and the Class mean row is the marker tray, a tray-tinted shelf with a 10px trim lip, the class means sitting on it. The class name is written at the top of the board at up to 8rem.
- **A class strip in the hero.** The hero's right half holds one line: every student's average as a dot on 0 to 100%, ticks at the grade boundaries with the grade written in each band, and a tick at the class average. Close averages stack, as equal marks do in the strip plots. It is ink and trim at rest; purple appears only on the dot that has just moved. Below 900px it sits under the summary. It has a text alternative with the lowest, highest and class average.
- **Strip plots instead of stat tiles.** Each assessment row carries a 120px dot strip of that assessment's marks, with a tick at the mean, and the text beside it says the same thing ("lowest 12, median 18.5, highest 26"). The open row shows a larger strip, a caption and the facts. The dots travel out from the mean tick once, on enter (S3). They are never scrubbed, so the plot is never wrong at rest.
- **Fast entry.** Up, Down and Enter always move between rows. Left and Right move between cells only when the caret is at the start or end of the input, or the whole value is selected, so caret movement inside a mark still works. Esc puts the stored mark back, Tab follows document order, and focusing a cell selects its value. Focus moves on the keypress itself; nothing waits for an animation. An invalid mark turns the cell border red, sets `aria-invalid`, and reveals a message under the row, linked by `aria-describedby`: "Ciara's mark must be a whole number from 0 to 30. Leave it blank if not handed in." A teacher tabbing through 30 marks never queues animations: each commit replaces any chain in flight on the same row or column (that chain is finished, and the shared figures restart their roll from the value on screen, with `overwrite: 'auto'` on the roll proxies), while chains on other rows and columns keep running. The live message is debounced 600ms, so only the latest commit is read. Rows do not re-sort under the teacher's hands; the order refreshes on the next sort, search or filter. The self-test makes three quick commits on the live page, settles the chains, and checks that the row average, column means and class average are final and that only the last message is queued.
- **CRUD with one schema.** `SCHEMA` lists every field of a student (text name, integer candidate number unique in the class, a choice of target grade 1 to 9, decimal attendance to one place, a joined date no later than today, a boolean for extra time), an assessment (title, kind, date set, out of 1 to 200, weight 0.5 to 5, counts toward the average) and a class (name, year group 7 to 11, room). That one list builds the dialog form, parses each type and supplies its error. Cross-field rules sit beside it: "4102 is already Tolu Adeyemi's number." and "Marks for this assessment go up to 26. Use 26 or more." Every record type is deleted the same way: a confirm dialog ("Delete Tolu Adeyemi? Their 5 marks go too. You can undo straight after.", "Delete Atomic structure test? 8 marks go too. You can undo straight after.", "Delete 10C Chemistry? 7 students and 28 marks go too. You can undo straight after."), then an untimed toast whose Undo restores the record with its marks, and a class with its students and marks. Undo is a stack: after two deletes the toast says "1 earlier delete can be undone after it", and each Undo restores the latest one. A student whose candidate number has been taken since the delete is not restored; the toast stays and the live region says "Candidate 4107 is now used by Kemi Oduya. Edit it first." A restored assessment goes back into date order. A stored book is used only if every class, student and assessment has its core fields; anything else falls back to the seed. Data lives in sessionStorage under `crud-gradebook:v1`, with every access in try/catch and an in-memory fallback. Reset is in the class menu and the footer.
- **390px is a list, not a scroller.** Below 900px each student is one row (name, average, target), and the accordion holds that student's mark inputs, each with its assessment title, the class mean and "/30", then the details. Nothing scrolls sideways.
- **Reasoned defaults.** The light ground follows from the whiteboard and from daytime marking. Headings are typed and rendered in sentence case; there are no eyebrows, middle dots, arrows or monospace labels. The rules carry meaning only: the board's aluminium bezel, the app bar's header rule, the grid header rule and the tray's ledge above Class mean, with no rules between student rows and none between sections (space separates them). Radii follow hierarchy: 4px chips, 8px buttons and fields, 10px menu, 14px frame and dialog, and fully round only for the switch and the grade pill. The only shadow is on the dialog sheet.
- **Cut at critique.** Three stat tiles in the hero (now one sentence whose figures roll); scroll entrances on the assessments and footer (the only scroll moments left carry data); scrubbed strip plots (wrong mid-scroll); scrambled candidate numbers; a cursor-replacing follower (now the ruler behind the row); hovering rows that lifted; Lexend and then Inclusive Sans, both now used by other designs for the same reading-ease reason; navy ink and #B4 red, too close to crud-ledger; distance-ordered ripples on the load rows, angled titles, menu items and dialog fields, which diluted the signature (TR3 now appears only in the wash and the strip dots, where distance means something); the edge lines on the column band, so only the row ruler keeps an edge; the S2 roll of every average and mean on scroll and the hero figure rolling on load (nothing had changed, so they spent the signature's language and showed wrong figures mid-roll); and the hairline rules between sections.

## Motion

All library motion lives in `gsap.matchMedia` with the conditions `motion`, `reduce`, `fine` and `wide` (900px). The house ease is expo.out (`--ease-out`) for entrances, power3.out (`--ease-follow`) for rolls and followers, and none for the scrub. Closes run at about 60% of the open duration. Lenis runs from `gsap.ticker` (`autoRaf: false`, lerp 0.1). It is stopped while the class menu or a dialog is open. After every accordion settles and after every Flip, `lenis.resize()` and `ScrollTrigger.refresh()` run. Anchors go through `lenis.scrollTo`, and focus then moves to the target. The first-frame hidden states exist only under `html.js.intro`. GSAP takes them over inline, and the class is removed if the CDN is blocked, after 2.5s, or under reduced motion.

## Do not touch

- **Purple means "just changed".** Do not use it for links, headings, charts or decoration. Green is ink and actions, red is errors, delete and below target, and rules are trim.
- **SC13 is reserved for figures that just changed.** Averages, means, the class average and grades roll only inside the signature chain or after a real data change (undo, delete, edit). Nothing rolls on load or on scroll: a roll there says "this changed" when nothing did, and shows wrong figures mid-roll.
- **TR3 is reserved.** Distance-ordered ripples appear only in the signature wash and the strip dots. Adding them to rows, menus or dialogs makes the signature one ripple among many.
- **Figures are computed.** Never type a number into the copy. Keep `round1` before `grade()` so the grade always matches the figure shown, keep blank (not handed in) different from 0, and keep the weighted-average formula in `studentAvg`.
- **The fast-entry contract.** One chain per commit, replaced (finished, not queued) by a later commit on the same row or column; rolls with `overwrite: 'auto'` that restart from the figure on screen; the 600ms live debounce; the delta measured from the last commit; focus moving on the keypress. Without these, a teacher typing at speed gets stacked animations and a stream of announcements.
- **Arrow-key rules in cells.** Left and Right move only from the caret's edge or a full selection, and `nextCell` stays covered by the self-test.
- **Angled titles stay pictures.** The upright cell is the target. Making the rotated text clickable brings back overlapping hit areas.
- **The grid's own scroll region.** Keep `role="region"`, the class-specific label, `data-lenis-prevent-horizontal`, the sticky name column and `scroll-padding-left`. Washes inside the grid stay inside its box, or the region gains a phantom scroll.
- **Flip with `absoluteOnLeave`, not `absolute`.** Absolutely positioning every row collapses the list mid-animation and drags the Class mean row over the students. The foot and empty state are Flip targets too.
- **Hidden first frames only under `html.js.intro`.** The grid's clip and the hero's opacity are set inline by GSAP before the class is removed.
- **Contrast** on the board: ink 13.6:1, ink-2 6.4:1, pen 5.1:1, purple 7.0:1 on the board (6.2:1 on the delta chip's 11% tint), red 5.0:1; trim 3.6:1 for borders. Tints were set so green and red text stay above 4.5:1 on them: the ruler at 8% trim (green 4.7:1, red 4.6:1), pen-tint at 8% (4.6:1), the menu's danger tint at 7% on white (4.8:1). Ink on the 22% purple wash is 9.6:1 and ink-2 4.5:1. Green on the wash (3.6:1) lasts only for the 0.6s pulse under the cell just committed. Mark inputs turn white on hover and focus, so a green mark there is 5.4:1. The tray tint (10% trim on the board) keeps ink above 11:1.

## Adapting

1. **Tokens first.** Map your brand's ink, ground and its one "done" colour onto `--ink`, `--board` and `--pen`. Keep `--progress` for change only and `--correct` for errors. Check that `--pen` and `--correct` text stay at 4.5:1 or more on `--ruler` and `--pen-tint`. If the new face has proportional figures, add `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` support or right-aligned fixed-width cells for the rolling figures.
2. **Your records.** Edit `SCHEMA`: a field's `type` (text, int, dec, date, bool, choice) picks its parser, input and error, and `full`, `hint` and `ui: 'seg'` shape the form. Cross-field checks live in `validateRecord`, confirm copy in `deleteCopy`. Replace `seed()` with your sample data, keep every derived figure computed, and switch `TODAY` to `new Date()` for real use.
3. **Your maths.** `studentAvg`, `assessMean`, `figures`, `BOUNDS` and `grade` hold all the arithmetic. For a points race or a budget, swap these and the formatter `pct`. The signature needs only to know which cells depend on which: the row's cells to its total, the column's cells to its mean.
4. **Copy.** Short column titles (under about 30 characters, so they stay inside the angled header) and names in "Given Family" order (`listName` shows "Family, Given"). Messages name the record and the result ("Deleted Tolu Adeyemi and 5 marks.", "Edit cancelled. Nothing changed.").
5. **Retime or remove motion.** Durations are tokens (`--dur-*`, `--step`, `--step-wash`, `--dur-roll`, `--dur-acc`). The script reads `--step`, `--step-wash`, `--dur-roll`, `--dur-acc` and `--dur-acc-close`. Every effect goes through the `fx` object, whose defaults are instant (`roll`, `propagate`, `flip`, `collapse`, `pulse`, `details`, `dialogIn`, `dialogOut`, `menuIn`, `menuOut`, `toastIn`, `saved`, `swapClass`, `replay`, `settle`, `scrollTo`, `lock`, `relayout`, `stripIn`, `washing`). Deleting an override in `motion()` therefore never breaks a flow. To slow the signature, raise `--step-wash`; to drop the load sequence, delete `intro()` and the `html.js.intro` rules. Removing GSAP and Lenis leaves a working page with CSS transitions only.
6. **Stack translation.** Keep the pure parts (schema, parsers, maths, record functions, `nextCell`, `deleteCopy`) apart from rendering, and run the GSAP layer in a `useGSAP` hook with `gsap.matchMedia`. Run `#selftest` after every change: it must log "SELFTEST PASS".
