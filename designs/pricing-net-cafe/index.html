Fine print

  • Under-18s cannot stay after 22:00. The law keeps under-18s out of PC rooms from 22:00 to 09:00, so staff check ID at 21:50 and the night pack is sold to adults only.
  • Cards are per person. A 30-hour card logs in one person at a time, in the booth type printed on it, and expires 60 days after its first login. Unused time is not refunded. A Couple booth card is sold to two named people and logs in both seats together.
  • The night pack ends at 08:00 whenever you started. If you stay on, the seat runs at the hourly price from 08:00.
  • Food comes to the seat. Ramyeon, rice bowls and drinks are ordered from the menu on your screen, brought to you, and added to your bill.
  • Couple booth prices cover both seats. One person in a Couple booth still pays the booth price.
  • A held seat waits 15 minutes past your arrival time, then goes back on the floor. Holding is free and you pay at the counter.
