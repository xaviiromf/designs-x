What the seed library does at each stage of the season
Stage Remarks Desk
Borrowed Take up to six packets a season from the drawers at any branch. Your Ashby library card is all you need, and there is no fee. Mar 21 2026
Sown This lot tested 9 of 10 sprouted in March, so sow at packet spacing. When a card shows fewer than 8 in 10, sow thicker. No stamp
Grown These beans climb past six feet, so give them a tall trellis. Bring questions to the Saturday seed clinic at the Main Street branch, 10 to noon, May to September. No stamp
Returned Bring back seeds from your two or three strongest plants, dry and in a labeled envelope. If the crop failed, return the card anyway and tell us what happened. We note it for the next grower. Sep 30 2026

Marked for beginners

Beans, peas, lettuce and tomatoes pollinate themselves, so the seed you save grows true. Their drawer labels say easy to save.

A history for every variety

The card file keeps every season a lot has spent in Ashby: who grew it, how it germinated and how much came back.

Envelopes and screens at the desk

Coin envelopes, labels and drying screens are free at every branch. Seed-cleaning night is the first Thursday of October.
