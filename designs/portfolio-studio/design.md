## Why it works

**The bold idea: each before/after is a real alteration plan.** Walls kept are grey poché, walls taken out are yellow with a dashed outline, and walls built new are red, as on a coloured-pencil alteration print. Doors (leaf and swing), windows (the opening and its glass line), stairs (treads and an arrow pointing up) and fixtures such as a kitchen counter are drawn as ink hairlines, so the plan reads as a drawing, not a diagram. A door or window can itself be taken out or new, and follows the same states. A three-stop control ("As we found it", "What we changed", "As it is now") moves the same plan through the job. Yellow walls fade out and red walls wipe in, so the "after" is drawn rather than photographed. The plan opens on Casa Miramar in "What we changed", so the convention is visible on load. All the colour on the page is spent on this drawing. Everything else is ink on green board.

- **One control panel, one print.** From 1024px up, the case list, the state control and the colour key share a sticky column beside the sheet, so the control that carries the idea is on the first screen at 1440x1000 and stays in reach while the plan scrolls. The sheet holds only what a real print holds: the drawing, its title block and the note. The drawing is capped at `min(64vh, 680px)` tall so the title block follows it closely. Below 1024px, the state control and key sit directly above the sheet. Below 720px the cases become a 2x2 grid of names (place and year move into the title block's "Where" cell), and red and yellow are on the first screen of a 390x844 phone.
- **Structure tells the truth.** The title block under the plan holds only real case facts: house, where and when, floor area, weeks on site, final cost against the quote, and drawing number. A scale bar (0, 1, 2, 5 m), computed from the wall units, replaces "1:100", which would be false on a screen. The visit steps are numbered because they are a sequence. The only other numbers are the room key, which replaces the plan labels when 13px names would collide with walls (phones).
- **One white sheet.** Only the plan is paper (`--sheet`, with a 1px ink frame inset 8px like a print border). Charts, form and contact rows sit straight on the board green, separated by space, and the chart plots have no paper behind them. Nothing reads as a card grid.
- **The figures open on their message.** The block's heading is computed from the data: "46 projects since 2018. 41 finished no more than 5% over the quote." It changes only when the type filter changes. One type filter, styled as the same dot radios as the case list, drives both charts.
- **Charts are ink only, and share one baseline.** In Work by year, each bar is split into solid ink (at most 5% over the quote) and an ink outline (more than 5% over). A two-item inline legend explains it. The current year carries a "to date" line under its tick, so a part year does not read as a decline. In Final cost against the quote, every project is a dot on a -10% to +15% axis, stacked where values collide, over a shaded band labelled "Within our promise". The two plots are drawn at the same height on a shared subgrid, so their x-axes sit on one line. Hover and focus only thicken a mark, add a ring and draw a value label at the mark, plus a hidden live line. They never add a colour, and there is no readout paragraph under either chart.
- **Copy comes from the data, and says what the rule is.** The promise is "no more than 5% over the quote" (`PROMISE`, used by `kept()`): a job that came in under the quote by any amount keeps it. The heading, the legend, the bar `aria-label`s, the hidden table header and the strip caption all use that wording. "The furthest out was 12% over: a rotten sill beam under Casa Gálvez" takes the `note` of the worst row. After an edit, none of these can disagree with the data, and the `#selftest` run asserts the exact heading, the legend and label wording, and that "within 5%" appears nowhere.
- **One family, three widths.** Encode Sans at wdth 118 gives headings the broad, even lettering of a drafting template. Wdth 87.5 fits room names and axis ticks into tight places, and 100 carries the body. The result has two voices without a second family. Tabular figures keep the title block and ticks aligned.
- **No dead ends.** "Book a site visit" is an anchor to `#visit`, which has `tabindex="-1"`. The case, state and type controls are native radio groups, each a single tab stop with arrow keys. Bars and the strip plot only reveal values. The form validates in JS (`novalidate`, `preventDefault`) with inline errors, then shows "Visit request prepared" in its place: a summary of the answers, "Send on WhatsApp" and "Send by email" links with the summary prefilled, and "Edit request", which restores the form with its values kept. The date must fall between tomorrow and a year from now, both computed in local time. A Saturday, Sunday or Monday is accepted, and the summary notes which visiting day we will suggest instead. Every external link opens in a new tab with `rel="noopener"`.
- **Cut at critique:** a Projects / Square metres toggle and filled filter chips (dashboard chrome), white plot panels behind the charts, the readout paragraphs under both charts, GitHub in the demo contacts (it stays available in `NETWORKS` for other trades), and an ink bar beside the selected case. Earlier passes also cut a KPI row, before/after photo cards, type-coloured stacked bars, a blueprint dark mode and round icon-only social buttons.

Contrast: ink `#1D2620` on board 10.3:1 and on sheet 15.0:1. Pencil `#48544D` on board 5.3:1 and on sheet 7.6:1. Red `#BC2A1F` is 5.8:1 on sheet but 4.0:1 on board, so it is never used for text on board. On board it marks only the error bar and the invalid border, which are non-text and above 3:1. Yellow (1.65:1) and kept grey (2.5:1) are always fills inside a 1.5px ink outline, which carries the contrast.

## Do not touch

- **Colour belongs to the drawing.** Red and yellow appear only where the alteration convention means them, plus red for the error bar and invalid border. Door, window and stair symbols stay ink. Chart marks stay ink, pencil and board. Do not colour bars by type.
- **The three states are CSS.** The script only sets `data-state` on the `.work` block. `found` fills remove-walls grey and hides new walls, symbols and fixtures. `changed` makes remove-walls yellow and dashed, new walls red, and fixture outlines red. `now` hides remove-walls and removed symbols and turns new walls grey. Keep this split, because the legend dimming and the transitions depend on it.
- **Layer order.** Taken-out and new walls are drawn under the kept walls, so kept poché covers their outline ends where they meet. Symbols and fixtures go on top.
- **Labels stay legible.** Room labels are drawn at 13px rendered size whatever the plan's scale. Below 4px per decimetre they switch to numbers with a key under the plan. Do not scale labels with the viewBox.
- **Copy computed from data stays computed:** the figures heading, the worst-case sentence, bar `aria-label`s, the live lines, both hidden tables, and the confirmation summary and its prefilled links.
- **Accessibility wiring.** The plan SVG has `role="img"`, an `aria-label` naming the house and state, and `aria-describedby` pointing to the case note, which says in words what was taken out and built. Each bar is a focusable `role="img"` with its full figures. The strip plot is one focusable `role="group"` container: Left/Right, Home and End step through the dots, sorted by cost against the quote. That container is never re-rendered, so focus survives redraws. The active dot's ring, leader and label ignore the pointer, so they never block the dots under them. Hidden tables are wrapped in a `.vh` div, because a table ignores `width: 1px` and would cause horizontal scroll. `[hidden]` is forced to `display: none` so component display rules cannot unhide the form or errors.
- **One white sheet, no shadows, no radius on the sheet.** Inputs, buttons and the segmented control keep 3px. No hover lift, scroll reveal, arrows on links, middle-dot meta strings, monospace or KPI tiles.

## Adapting

1. **Contact and social links.** Edit `CONTACT` at the top of the script. Links render in object order. Delete a key to hide that link (and the matching send link in the form confirmation). Known keys are `whatsapp` (country code and number; spaces and `+` are stripped), `email`, `instagram` and `github` (handles, with or without `@`) and `linkedin` (a path such as `company/your-firm` or `in/your-name`). Any value may also be a full https URL, which is used as is. To add any other network, add one line whose value is a full https URL, for example `facebook: 'https://www.facebook.com/yourstudio'` (as in the demo) or `behance: 'https://www.behance.net/yourstudio'`. It gets its key as the label, and an icon from `ICON` if one has that key, otherwise a generic link icon. An unknown key whose value is not an https URL is skipped. If both `whatsapp` and `email` are missing, the confirmation says "Copy these details into a message to us" and shows no send links.
2. **Chart data.** Edit `PROJECTS`, one line per finished job: `{ year, type, m2, vsQuote }`, where `type` is a key of `TYPES` and `vsQuote` is the final account against the signed quote in percent (negative is under). Add `note` to a row to have it named when it is the furthest over. Years between the first and last row get a bar even when empty. The latest year is labelled "to date" while it is the current year. To change the promise, edit `PROMISE`: the wording, the split and the band follow. To add a type, add a line to `TYPES` (plural filter label, singular phrase).
3. **Cases.** Edit `CASES`. Plan units are decimetres. Each wall is `[x1, y1, x2, y2, 'keep' | 'remove' | 'new' | 'fixture', thickness]`, with thickness defaulting to 1.5 (15 cm); pass 3 or 5 for exterior walls. A gap is an opening and a zero-length wall is a post. A fixture is outlined only (red in "What we changed"). `doors` are `[hingeX, hingeY, width, angle, swing, status]`: the angle points from the hinge across the opening, and swing (1 or -1) picks the side the leaf opens to. `windows` are `[x1, y1, x2, y2, status]` on an exterior wall: the window cuts the wall itself, a new window in a kept wall leaves that stretch as a taken-out wall in "What we changed", and a window with no wall under it (a glazed end) takes a sixth item for its thickness. `stairs` are `[x, y, w, h, treads, 'up' | 'down' | 'left' | 'right', status]`, the direction being the way up. Each room is `[x, y, label]`, with an optional fourth item `'found'` or `'now'` for labels that exist only before or after. "What we changed" uses the "now" labels, so keep those clear of yellow walls and door swings. The viewBox, scale bar and title block are computed. Keep each note to two sentences: what was taken out, then what was built.
4. **Tokens.** Map a brand onto the `:root` colours by role: a mid-tone ground, one light sheet, one dark ink, one secondary grey, and the two convention colours (if your trade has its own convention, use that instead, but keep it the only colour). If you replace Encode Sans, choose a family with a real width axis and tabular figures, or use two widths of a superfamily.
5. **A real backend later.** Load `PROJECTS` and `CASES` as JSON with the same shape (from a spreadsheet export or your job-costing system), assign them, then call `renderCase()` and `renderCharts()`. To receive requests directly, keep the form's `submit` handler, `fetch` the collected `items` to your endpoint, and show the same confirmation panel with a "Sent" line instead of the send links. Never give the form an `action`, because static hosts answer POST with 405.
6. **Self-test.** Open the page with `#selftest` and the console prints `SELFTEST PASS` or the failures. It checks the figures heading and strip caption against `PROJECTS`, the promise wording, that doors, windows and the stair are drawn, that removing and adding CONTACT keys works and that loosely typed values (`+56 9 ...`, `@handle`, a full URL) still give clean links, that plan labels are at least 12px, and that the form goes empty submit, a date too far out, valid submit, then edit. `scripts/check-flows.mjs` runs it.
7. **Stack translation.** In React or Svelte, keep `CASES`, `PROJECTS` and `CONTACT` as plain modules, hold the case, plan state and type as the only state, keep `data-state` plus CSS for the plan, and redraw the charts at a width measured with a ResizeObserver rather than scaling a viewBox, so ticks keep their size.

Chart geometry in the script (margins, the shared 220/260px plot height, the 40px axis margin, 4-5px dot radius, 58% bar width capped at 44px) is literal on purpose: it is drawing and depends on the label size.
