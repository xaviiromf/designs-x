Version 2.11.3, MIT licence, Node 20 and later

Client work

Naviera Austral

Ferry bookings on the Puerto Montt to Chaitén route failed whenever a passenger lost signal halfway through checkout.

I rebuilt booking offline-first: the cart lives on the phone and confirms itself when the connection comes back. Its checkout rules became pliego 2.0.

Checkout failures down from 11% to 2%.

Vinoteca Itata

A wine merchant with three shops in Concepción kept stock in one shared spreadsheet and kept selling bottles it no longer had.

I built a stock and orders app that keeps the three tills in sync and drafts reorders to the Itata valley growers. The async stock checks became pliego 3.0.

Oversold orders down from about 30 a month to 2.

Centro Médico Andalué

Patients filled in the same paper intake form at every visit, and the front desk typed it all in again.

I replaced it with online intake forms that pliego checks in the browser and again on the server, with reminders sent by ascensor. The reminder scheduler became pliego 4.0's server rules and ascensor's cron.

Check-in down from 9 minutes to 3, and no more retyping.
